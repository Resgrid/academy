Communication Tests

Test messages that show whether members can be reached.

Why it helps
Collect observed test evidence; registration alone does not prove delivery.

How to configure it
Run a communication test before going live and after major changes.

Before you begin
Check your department access and intended audience.

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Communication Tests: Review eligible channels and intended team recipients before an authorized communication test; configuration alone does not prove delivery.
Search and rescue: Communication Tests: Review volunteer alert channels and inactive members; test reviewed recipients and fallback communications.
Security companies and departments: Communication Tests: Review site/patrol alert recipients and channel preferences; prove delivery through a reviewed test.
Wildland contractors: Communication Tests: Review crew communication channels and remote-connectivity limitations before mobilization.
Industrial emergency response teams: Communication Tests: Review on-duty facility recipients and specialist escalation channels; test reviewed recipients and fallback procedures.
