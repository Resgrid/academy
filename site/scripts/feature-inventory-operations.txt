Inventory counts, expiry and alerts

Counts, expiry dates and low-stock alerts.

Why it helps
Identify inventory data and supplies that need owner follow-up.

How to configure it
Set minimum levels and expiry alerts for critical supplies.

Before you begin
Inventory module enabled

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Inventory counts, expiry and alerts: Track operational kits and consumables by location; record issues and returns without exposing clinical information.
Search and rescue: Inventory counts, expiry and alerts: Track rope, radios and search kits; issue equipment to a team, then reconcile returns and damaged items.
Security companies and departments: Inventory counts, expiry and alerts: Track radios, PPE and site keys as appropriate to inventory policy; reconcile equipment issued to patrol staff.
Wildland contractors: Inventory counts, expiry and alerts: Track tools, hose and consumables by base or crew; record transfers and returns after an assignment.
Industrial emergency response teams: Inventory counts, expiry and alerts: Track PPE, spill supplies and response kits; review stock, expiry and damaged equipment.
