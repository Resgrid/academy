Big Board

A station display of calls, units and personnel.

Why it helps
Choose display refresh, location age and visibility appropriate to a station screen.

How to configure it
Open the Big Board on a station screen and choose what it shows.

Before you begin
Check your department access and intended audience.

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Big Board: Choose member, dispatch and display apps according to response-team duties; verify device registration and external providers separately.
Search and rescue: Big Board: Choose field-member, dispatch and coordination-display apps; verify location update and remote connectivity.
Security companies and departments: Big Board: Choose patrol-member, dispatcher and site-display apps; verify site scope and device registration.
Wildland contractors: Big Board: Choose crew-member, dispatch and coordination apps; verify device updates during remote assignments.
Industrial emergency response teams: Big Board: Choose responder, control-room and display apps; verify provider prerequisites and facility connectivity.
