Inventory purchasing

Suppliers, purchase orders and deliveries.

Why it helps
Connect replenishment to recorded stock needs and authorized receipt.

How to configure it
Decide which supplies and equipment are worth tracking and where they are stored.

Before you begin
Inventory module enabled

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Inventory purchasing: Track operational kits and consumables by location; record issues and returns without exposing clinical information.
Search and rescue: Inventory purchasing: Track rope, radios and search kits; issue equipment to a team, then reconcile returns and damaged items.
Security companies and departments: Inventory purchasing: Track radios, PPE and site keys as appropriate to inventory policy; reconcile equipment issued to patrol staff.
Wildland contractors: Inventory purchasing: Track tools, hose and consumables by base or crew; record transfers and returns after an assignment.
Industrial emergency response teams: Inventory purchasing: Track PPE, spill supplies and response kits; review stock, expiry and damaged equipment.
