NARRATION: Welcome to Resgrid Academy. In this lesson, we will put the right resgrid app on each device. ADAPT THIS WORKFLOW TO YOUR DEPARTMENT (written companion):
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Choose member, dispatch and display apps according to response-team duties; verify device registration and external providers separately.
Search and rescue: Choose field-member, dispatch and coordination-display apps; verify location update and remote connectivity.
Security companies and departments: Choose patrol-member, dispatcher and site-display apps; verify site scope and device registration.
Wildland contractors: Choose crew-member, dispatch and coordination apps; verify device updates during remote assignments.
Industrial emergency response teams: Choose responder, control-room and display apps; verify provider prerequisites and facility connectivity.
