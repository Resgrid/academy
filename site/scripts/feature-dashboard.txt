Dashboard

The department home page: current status, staffing and activity.

Why it helps
Most members use Resgrid through the Responder app. Getting everyone installed and signed in is what lets dispatches reach them.

How to configure it
Decide which devices each role will use and share the app download links with your members.

Before you begin
Check your department access and intended audience.

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Dashboard: Choose member, dispatch and display apps according to response-team duties; verify device registration and external providers separately.
Search and rescue: Dashboard: Choose field-member, dispatch and coordination-display apps; verify location update and remote connectivity.
Security companies and departments: Dashboard: Choose patrol-member, dispatcher and site-display apps; verify site scope and device registration.
Wildland contractors: Dashboard: Choose crew-member, dispatch and coordination apps; verify device updates during remote assignments.
Industrial emergency response teams: Dashboard: Choose responder, control-room and display apps; verify provider prerequisites and facility connectivity.
