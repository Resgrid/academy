NERIS incident analysis lifecycle

Incident analysis is a separately validated and submitted artifact linked to an incident report.

Why it helps
Incident analysis is a separately validated and submitted artifact linked to an incident report.

How to configure it
Start analysis from a demo incident, edit and validate it, then review finalization and submission readiness.

Before you begin
Check your department access and intended audience.

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: NERIS incident analysis lifecycle: Use an approved operational record definition for visits or responses; select fields and access according to the service’s record policy.
Search and rescue: NERIS incident analysis lifecycle: Record search activity, participants and outcomes; distinguish a saved draft from a reviewed or finalized record.
Security companies and departments: NERIS incident analysis lifecycle: Record patrol or incident activity under an appropriate definition; review required fields and client/site access.
Wildland contractors: NERIS incident analysis lifecycle: Record assignment activity and crew evidence; link the correct call or deployment and retain review status.
Industrial emergency response teams: NERIS incident analysis lifecycle: Record drills and response activity; review the definition, participants and finalization requirements.
