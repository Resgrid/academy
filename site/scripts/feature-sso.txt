Single sign-on and provisioning

Let members sign in through your identity provider, with optional automatic account provisioning.

Why it helps
Review identity mapping, local fallback and recovery before enforcing SSO.

How to configure it
Know your department address and time zone, and choose at least two people who will administer Resgrid.

Before you begin
Check your department access and intended audience.

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Single sign-on and provisioning: Set the local time zone for scheduled outreach visits; review whether a daily reset would incorrectly mark an occupied response team available.
Search and rescue: Single sign-on and provisioning: Choose availability reset rules that suit volunteers and overnight searches; avoid implying that a remote volunteer has arrived at staging.
Security companies and departments: Single sign-on and provisioning: Set patrol-day boundaries and display order; review staffing suppression before excluding an off-duty patrol member from alerts.
Wildland contractors: Single sign-on and provisioning: Review reset times across overnight assignments and the deployment time zone before changing contractor crew availability.
Industrial emergency response teams: Single sign-on and provisioning: Align display time with plant shifts and review automatic resets before overwriting a team engaged in an extended incident.
