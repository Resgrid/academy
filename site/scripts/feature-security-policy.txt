Security and session policy

Two-factor requirements, password rules and session limits.

Why it helps
Review lockout and recovery risks with an enrolled administrator.

How to configure it
Require two-factor sign-in for administrators and review password and session rules.

Before you begin
Check your department access and intended audience.

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Security and session policy: Set the local time zone for scheduled outreach visits; review whether a daily reset would incorrectly mark an occupied response team available.
Search and rescue: Security and session policy: Choose availability reset rules that suit volunteers and overnight searches; avoid implying that a remote volunteer has arrived at staging.
Security companies and departments: Security and session policy: Set patrol-day boundaries and display order; review staffing suppression before excluding an off-duty patrol member from alerts.
Wildland contractors: Security and session policy: Review reset times across overnight assignments and the deployment time zone before changing contractor crew availability.
Industrial emergency response teams: Security and session policy: Align display time with plant shifts and review automatic resets before overwriting a team engaged in an extended incident.
