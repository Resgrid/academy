Documents

Shared documents such as procedures and preplans.

Why it helps
Members find the current procedure, preplan or phone number when they need it instead of searching email.

How to configure it
Upload your most-used documents and organize them by category.

Before you begin
Documents module enabled

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Documents: Organize approved response and referral procedures with responsible owners and review dates.
Search and rescue: Documents: Organize search, navigation and equipment protocols and mutual-aid references.
Security companies and departments: Documents: Organize site instructions, escalation procedures and patrol reference documents.
Wildland contractors: Documents: Organize assignment, equipment and agency procedures with seasonal review ownership.
Industrial emergency response teams: Documents: Organize facility response plans, access instructions and equipment procedures with current owners.
