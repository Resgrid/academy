Incident reports

Incident reports linked to calls.

Why it helps
Review source completeness before finalization or external submission.

How to configure it
Decide who completes and who reviews each report.

Before you begin
Feature access enabled by your system operator

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Incident reports: Use an approved operational record definition for visits or responses; select fields and access according to the service’s record policy.
Search and rescue: Incident reports: Record search activity, participants and outcomes; distinguish a saved draft from a reviewed or finalized record.
Security companies and departments: Incident reports: Record patrol or incident activity under an appropriate definition; review required fields and client/site access.
Wildland contractors: Incident reports: Record assignment activity and crew evidence; link the correct call or deployment and retain review status.
Industrial emergency response teams: Incident reports: Record drills and response activity; review the definition, participants and finalization requirements.
