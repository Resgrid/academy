New Checklist

Create a checklist.

Why it helps
Recorded checks show what was inspected and what was missed.

How to configure it
Pick the checks you do on paper today and who completes them.

Before you begin
Feature access enabled by your system operator

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: New Checklist: Use a vehicle or operational-kit checklist; follow up on failed items and distinguish scheduled compliance from an on-demand check.
Search and rescue: New Checklist: Use search-kit and vehicle readiness checks; assign failed equipment follow-up.
Security companies and departments: New Checklist: Use patrol vehicle or site-opening checks; review failures and missed scheduled checks.
Wildland contractors: New Checklist: Use crew and apparatus readiness checks before mobilization; create accountable defect follow-up.
Industrial emergency response teams: New Checklist: Use response-kit and equipment checks; review required items and route failures to maintenance.
