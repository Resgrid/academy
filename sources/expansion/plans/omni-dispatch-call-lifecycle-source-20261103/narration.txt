In Omni Dispatch, open New Call for a community outreach standby. Enter a name your team will recognize and use Nature to describe what responders need to know. This exercise uses Medical and Routine. Choose the type and priority your department uses for this work.

On the location section, select GPS and enter the latitude and longitude. Check the map against your intended site. A search team can use coordinates for a trail rendezvous, and an industrial response team can use them for an access gate.

Use the contact picker if an existing department contact belongs on the call. Here we select Academy Community Partners. Check Save as pending and select Save Pending Call. The call now has a number in Pending Calls, where dispatch can find it again when it is ready for release.

Open the pending call and start dispatch. In the recipient picker, select the intended recipient. Here we choose Wallace Boden. Check the selection count on Done before confirming. Back in the console, find the same call number under Active Calls and open Details.

Select Notes and enter an update that will make sense to the next dispatcher. Save it, then verify that the entry appears on the call. A security desk could record when site staff completed a handoff, and a wildland contractor could record where the relief crew should report.

Use Call actions and Edit Call to update the incident description. Verify the revised wording on the call. When the exercise is over, choose Close Call, confirm the reason and enter a closeout note. Review Notify everyone who was dispatched before confirming. In this training exercise we leave it off.

Back in the console, search for the call and verify that it has left the active queue. In this exercise we can also open History on a nearby call. Match the original call number and read its Closed status under Previous calls at this location.
