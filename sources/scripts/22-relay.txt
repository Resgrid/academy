Relay: bring an audio dispatch feed into Resgrid
Audience: System operators and dispatch administrators
reference-based illustrated lesson; live walkthrough pending

SCENE 1: Connect the existing dispatch feed
ON SCREEN: Scanner or audio feed | Tone detection and captured dispatch audio
NARRATION: Resgrid Relay listens to an audio feed, detects configured tones, records dispatch audio, and creates calls for the associated department or groups. It can help departments connect an existing paging process when they cannot modify the upstream dispatch system.

SCENE 2: Prepare a controlled audio test
ON SCREEN: Identify the approved tone pair | Connect and check the audio input | Review recording and recipient configuration
NARRATION: The recording plan begins with an approved sample audio feed and known tone frequencies. Check the input signal, configure the intended recording behavior, and review the department or group recipients. Verify the exact installer and interface against the supported Relay release before capture.

SCENE 3: Verify the resulting call
ON SCREEN: Play a synthetic tone-and-voice test | Inspect the call, captured audio, and recipients
NARRATION: Use synthetic test audio. Inspect the resulting call, the captured recording, and the intended recipients. Test nonmatching tones separately and document what was observed. The public Relay installation page contains incomplete older steps, so it is a feature reference rather than a verified modern installation procedure.

SCENE 4: Keep the test repeatable
ON SCREEN: Retain a sample audio fixture | Record configuration, release, and observed result
NARRATION: Retain the synthetic audio fixture, selected release, approved configuration, and observed test result. A recorder will need the supported Relay application and an audio test setup to turn this briefing into a live walkthrough.

ADAPT THIS WORKFLOW TO YOUR DEPARTMENT (written companion):
Community paramedicine and mental health response: If an approved audio dispatch feed exists, configure an appropriate reviewed relay source; validate transcription and recipient selection.
Search and rescue: Review the authorized dispatch audio source, transcription quality and search-team classification before using Relay.
Security companies and departments: Review whether the organization has an appropriate authorized audio feed; validate site classification and recipients.
Wildland contractors: Review the authorized assignment/dispatch feed and remote connection prerequisites; verify parsed output.
Industrial emergency response teams: Review the authorized control-room dispatch feed and provider prerequisites; verify parsed incident details.
