Module settings

Turn optional modules on or off for the department.

Why it helps
Reduce navigation clutter while understanding retained data and add-on prerequisites.

How to configure it
Hide modules you will not use so members see a simpler menu.

Before you begin
Check your department access and intended audience.

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Module settings: Set the local time zone for scheduled outreach visits; review whether a daily reset would incorrectly mark an occupied response team available.
Search and rescue: Module settings: Choose availability reset rules that suit volunteers and overnight searches; avoid implying that a remote volunteer has arrived at staging.
Security companies and departments: Module settings: Set patrol-day boundaries and display order; review staffing suppression before excluding an off-duty patrol member from alerts.
Wildland contractors: Module settings: Review reset times across overnight assignments and the deployment time zone before changing contractor crew availability.
Industrial emergency response teams: Module settings: Align display time with plant shifts and review automatic resets before overwriting a team engaged in an extended incident.
