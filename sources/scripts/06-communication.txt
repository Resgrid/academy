NARRATION: Welcome to Resgrid Academy. In this lesson, we will make alerts reach the intended audience. ADAPT THIS WORKFLOW TO YOUR DEPARTMENT (written companion):
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Review eligible channels and intended team recipients before an authorized communication test; configuration alone does not prove delivery.
Search and rescue: Review volunteer alert channels and inactive members; test reviewed recipients and fallback communications.
Security companies and departments: Review site/patrol alert recipients and channel preferences; prove delivery through a reviewed test.
Wildland contractors: Review crew communication channels and remote-connectivity limitations before mobilization.
Industrial emergency response teams: Review on-duty facility recipients and specialist escalation channels; test reviewed recipients and fallback procedures.
