Invite a member and verify their completed registration

Save a department invitation, inspect the delivered training email, register a fictional member, and verify completion and the new personnel row.

Audience: Department administrators, Personnel managers, New department members

Before you begin

  • Authorized personnel administration in an isolated fictional training department.
  • A fictional recipient and a controlled training mailbox for following the invitation exercise.

Chapters

  1. Open department invitations
  2. Prepare and save the intended invitation
  3. Check pending status and actual email delivery
  4. Complete the member’s registration form
  5. Verify the signed-in member workspace
  6. Verify completion as the administrator
  7. Find the resulting member in Personnel
  8. Use invitation readbacks across department types

Watch the walkthrough

Download video · Download captions

Step 1

Open department invitations

From Personnel, select Manage Invites. The Invites page separates the invitation history from the Send Invites form. Its history has Email Address, Sent On and Completed On columns. This walkthrough invites a fictional new member, Derrick Gibson, and verifies the result from both sides. An invitation is useful when a new team member should complete their own account details instead of an administrator entering every credential through Add a Person.

Practice

Open department invitations

Step 2

Prepare and save the intended invitation

Enter the intended recipient in Email Addresses. The help explains that multiple addresses can be separated by commas. This exercise uses a single fictional training address. Review the destination and select Send Invites. The saved row then shows the address, a Sent On time and Pending under Completed On. Check that row for the intended recipient. Pending is an invitation state; it does not establish that the recipient has finished joining the department.

Practice

Prepare and save the intended invitation

Step 3

Check pending status and actual email delivery

While the invitation is pending, the row offers Resend Invite. If delivery needs another attempt, review the intended address and confirm that the recipient actually receives the invitation. Here the controlled local Mailpit inbox contains the Resgrid invitation for the training department. Opening that email shows the inviting administrator, the department and a Set up account button. This mailbox is our local recording tool; your members use their own mailbox. Follow Set up account to the registration form. The invitation link itself is private and is not shown in this lesson.

Practice

Check pending status and actual email delivery

Step 4

Complete the member’s registration form

The form identifies the department the recipient is joining. Enter the member’s own name, a unique Username, and matching password fields. Our fictional example is Derrick Gibson, and the passwords remain masked. The displayed password requirement is at least eight characters, including a digit, an uppercase letter and a lowercase letter. Review the terms of use and privacy policy notice before Register, because signing up accepts those terms. The notice also describes existing-account linking, but this exercise creates a new fictional account. Select Register after that review.

Practice

Complete the member’s registration form

Step 5

Verify the signed-in member workspace

The returned workspace is signed in as Derrick Gibson. Check the name at the upper left and the intended department in the header. That visible signed-in result is stronger evidence than assuming the Register button worked. The new member now has a department workspace in this local exercise. Operational status and staffing controls are separate from accepting the invitation; no responding status or staffing change is performed here.

Practice

Verify the signed-in member workspace

Step 6

Verify completion as the administrator

Return to Manage Invites under the administrator account. The same recipient now has an actual Completed On date and time rather than Pending. Sent On remains a separate value. Compare those two columns when checking whether an invitation was merely sent or was completed. In this recorded example, completion is saved at seventeen fifteen and fifty-six seconds on the displayed date. This administrative readback confirms the completed invitation independently of the member’s signed-in page.

Practice

Verify completion as the administrator

Step 7

Find the resulting member in Personnel

Open Personnel and use Search to find Derrick Gibson. The saved row shows Normal under State, Available under Staffing and Standing By under Status. Roles is still blank for this newly invited member. Review the department group and operational role the person needs as part of your own onboarding process. The member-access walkthrough demonstrates those separate controls. This invitation exercise verifies membership without granting a department administrator role or assigning an operational role.

Practice

Find the resulting member in Personnel

Step 8

Use invitation readbacks across department types

Use the same invitation and verification process for community paramedicine and mental health response teams, search-and-rescue organizations, security departments, wildland contractors and industrial emergency teams, as well as fire and E M S departments. Send to the intended recipient, verify delivery, let the member complete the form, and check the completed invitation and personnel row. Those readbacks help administrators distinguish an outstanding invitation from a member who has actually joined.
