Advanced Data Protection

The Advanced Data Protection add-on: extra encryption and controlled access for sensitive data.

Why it helps
Control protected content disclosure and recovery through the department protection lifecycle.

How to configure it
Buying does not enroll the department. Review MFA, recovery, migration and integration behavior; this is not a compliance certification.

Before you begin
Advanced Data Protection add-on access

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Advanced Data Protection: Review protected-record access, key and recovery prerequisites according to the service’s policy.
Search and rescue: Advanced Data Protection: Review protected operational details and recovery ownership; verify authorized access.
Security companies and departments: Advanced Data Protection: Review client/site protected-data access and recovery responsibilities.
Wildland contractors: Advanced Data Protection: Review protected assignment data and key/recovery ownership.
Industrial emergency response teams: Advanced Data Protection: Review protected facility information and recovery procedures with responsible owners.
