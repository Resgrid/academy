Checklist schedules and due work

Recurring schedules and due checks.

Why it helps
Make recorded equipment and procedure checks visible to their owners.

How to configure it
Schedule each checklist and choose who completes it.

Before you begin
Checklists module enabled

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Checklist schedules and due work: Use a vehicle or operational-kit checklist; follow up on failed items and distinguish scheduled compliance from an on-demand check.
Search and rescue: Checklist schedules and due work: Use search-kit and vehicle readiness checks; assign failed equipment follow-up.
Security companies and departments: Checklist schedules and due work: Use patrol vehicle or site-opening checks; review failures and missed scheduled checks.
Wildland contractors: Checklist schedules and due work: Use crew and apparatus readiness checks before mobilization; create accountable defect follow-up.
Industrial emergency response teams: Checklist schedules and due work: Use response-kit and equipment checks; review required items and route failures to maintenance.
