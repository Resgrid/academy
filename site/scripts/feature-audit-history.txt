Audit history

See who changed department settings and when.

Why it helps
Identify what changed and who should verify the effect.

How to configure it
Know your department address and time zone, and choose at least two people who will administer Resgrid.

Before you begin
Check your department access and intended audience.

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Audit history: Set the local time zone for scheduled outreach visits; review whether a daily reset would incorrectly mark an occupied response team available.
Search and rescue: Audit history: Choose availability reset rules that suit volunteers and overnight searches; avoid implying that a remote volunteer has arrived at staging.
Security companies and departments: Audit history: Set patrol-day boundaries and display order; review staffing suppression before excluding an off-duty patrol member from alerts.
Wildland contractors: Audit history: Review reset times across overnight assignments and the deployment time zone before changing contractor crew availability.
Industrial emergency response teams: Audit history: Align display time with plant shifts and review automatic resets before overwriting a team engaged in an extended incident.
