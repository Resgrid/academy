ADAPT THIS WORKFLOW TO YOUR DEPARTMENT (written companion):
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Model response areas or outreach teams as groups and mobile response vehicles as units; define team positions appropriate to the service.
Search and rescue: Model bases and field teams as groups and search vehicles or boats as units; define team leader and operator positions.
Security companies and departments: Model clients or sites as groups and patrol vehicles as units; check membership before applying site-scoped dispatch.
Wildland contractors: Model bases and crews as groups and engines or support vehicles as units; require the appropriate driver or crew positions.
Industrial emergency response teams: Model facilities or plants as groups and response vehicles or equipment as units; define operators and response-team positions.
