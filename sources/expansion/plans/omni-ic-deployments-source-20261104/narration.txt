Open Deployments in Omni IC and choose Open. Find the training deployment by name and check its status and date range. Here, Community Support is Demobilizing, so this is a good time to review the crew's paperwork.

On the deployment, check the date and the selected crew. A missing report applies to that crew and date. You can start a report where your permissions allow it, or change the date to review one that already exists.

Open the Date picker and select November second for this exercise. The date is part of the report's scope, so confirm it before reviewing the hours.

Choose Done to apply the date. The crew is the same, but the report list now shows the entries for that day.

This crew report is marked Approved and totals eleven and a half hours. Compare the date, crew, total, and status together. That helps you avoid treating a draft for another day as the approved record.

The next day's report is a draft. In the note field for the intended entry, write a short note that explains the change. Here we note a fictional rehearsal check-in. A wildland contractor or an industrial response team can use this same field to explain a crew entry.

Choose Save entries to keep the change in the draft. Submit report is a separate action. Leave the draft available for review until the required checks are complete.

The screen confirms that the entries were saved. The report still says Draft. That gives the next reviewer the updated details and a clear indication that approval has not happened.

Reopen the report and look for the note on the same crew entry. This final check is useful whenever work changes hands, from a search team demobilizing to a community response team closing out a standby.
