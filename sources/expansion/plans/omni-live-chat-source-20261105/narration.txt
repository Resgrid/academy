In Omni Dispatch, open Chat. Direct Messages lists your individual conversations. Channels and incident rooms are listed separately. For this exercise, select Matthew Casey.

Check the name at the top before you write. This is a direct conversation with Casey, who has Omni I C open.

Use the message field to say what is ready and what you need the recipient to do. Here, Dispatch says Command can review the archived station inspection. Leave Urgent off for this routine update and select Send.

The message now appears in the Dispatch conversation. That shows it was sent from this console. Check the recipient’s screen for delivery.

On the I C console, Casey opens the direct conversation with Wallace Boden. Here is the conversation before the new update.

The inspection update appears in I C while the conversation stays open. This confirms live delivery in the app. A device push alert is a separate check and depends on the department’s push setup.

Casey replies that the update arrived and that they will review the inspection. In a real handoff, state what you received and what you are taking responsibility for.

Back in Dispatch, open the conversation to confirm that the acknowledgment is saved. This gives both operators a record of the handoff. The same pattern works for a search base, a security desk, or a field support team.
