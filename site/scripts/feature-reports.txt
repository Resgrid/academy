Reports

Department reports on calls, personnel and activity.

Why it helps
Keep incident and activity records with the calls they came from, ready for review and reporting.

How to configure it
Run the reports you use for meetings or compliance.

Before you begin
Reports module enabled

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Reports: Use an approved operational record definition for visits or responses; select fields and access according to the service’s record policy.
Search and rescue: Reports: Record search activity, participants and outcomes; distinguish a saved draft from a reviewed or finalized record.
Security companies and departments: Reports: Record patrol or incident activity under an appropriate definition; review required fields and client/site access.
Wildland contractors: Reports: Record assignment activity and crew evidence; link the correct call or deployment and retain review status.
Industrial emergency response teams: Reports: Record drills and response activity; review the definition, participants and finalization requirements.
