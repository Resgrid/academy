New Calendar Event

Add an event to the department calendar.

Why it helps
Shifts show who is on duty and can drive dispatch, so calls page the people who are actually working.

How to configure it
Have your rotation or duty schedule, and decide whether dispatch should follow shifts.

Before you begin
Calendar module enabled

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: New Calendar Event: Schedule mobile-crisis or outreach coverage windows and eligible team roles; review gaps and overlapping assignments.
Search and rescue: New Calendar Event: Schedule search standby and field-team coverage with eligible roles; do not confuse sign-up with arrival at staging.
Security companies and departments: New Calendar Event: Schedule site patrol coverage and supervisor positions; review whether members may sign up for multiple shift groups.
Wildland contractors: New Calendar Event: Schedule crew availability and relief windows; check conflicts with deployments and rest requirements.
Industrial emergency response teams: New Calendar Event: Schedule facility ERT coverage across plant shifts; review required positions and overlapping assignments.
