Readiness Pro

The Readiness Pro add-on: work orders, preventive maintenance, repair approvals and safety holds.

Why it helps
Give equipment defects an owner and a recorded repair and return-to-service review.

How to configure it
Checklists do not require this add-on. Maintenance needs its module, permissions and active entitlement. Historical evidence and hold release follow owning-module rules.

Before you begin
Readiness Pro add-on access

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Readiness Pro: Turn a response-vehicle or kit defect into a work order with an owner, state and completion evidence.
Search and rescue: Readiness Pro: Track defective radios or search equipment through review, repair and return to service.
Security companies and departments: Readiness Pro: Track patrol equipment and vehicle defects through assigned repairs and verification.
Wildland contractors: Readiness Pro: Track apparatus and tool defects with assignment impact and return-to-service evidence.
Industrial emergency response teams: Readiness Pro: Track response-equipment defects through repair ownership and verified completion.
