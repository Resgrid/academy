Turn defects into accountable repairs
Welcome to Resgrid Academy. In this lesson, we will turn defects into accountable repairs. A defect reported in chat can be forgotten when it has no work order, owner, approval, or return-to-service record. We will explain the features, the setup decisions, and a practical way to check the result.

A department scenario
A failed pump test creates a work order, and the engine stays on a safety hold until the repair is approved. Turn a failed check or reported defect into a tracked repair and a documented return to service. The examples in this series use a fictional department and are teaching scenarios, not default settings.

Maintenance history and reports. Maintenance history, repair costs and safety holds. Requires the Readiness Pro add-on. Checklists remain available without it. Availability also depends on Readiness Pro, Maintenance. Check access in the target department.

Readiness Pro
Readiness Pro. The Readiness Pro add-on: work orders, preventive maintenance, repair approvals and safety holds. Checklists do not require this add-on. Maintenance needs its module, permissions and active entitlement. Historical evidence and hold release follow owning-module rules. Availability also depends on Readiness Pro. Parts, vendor charges, and approvals
Work-order operations connect parts, vendor charges, approval requests, and spending policy to a repair. Review policy, reserve a sample part, record its movement and a vendor charge, then request the required approval. Reconcile repair cost and part movement with the work order and approval decision. Confirm availability in the demo department before recording the live workflow.

Confirm the preview, current revisions, and the observed result after an authorized demo apply. Confirm availability in the demo department before recording the live workflow.

Configure one useful workflow
For the training scenario, work in this order. Step 1. Confirm Readiness Pro access, the Maintenance module, and permissions. Step 2. Create a work order from a reported defect and assign responsibility. Step 3. Record corrective work and follow the configured approval and safety-hold process.

Verify the result
Now verify the result. Review the repair history, recorded costs, approval state, and any safety hold. Release follows the owning maintenance workflow. A common problem to watch for: Checklists are available separately from Readiness Pro. Buying maintenance access does not configure the module or approval policy.

Try it with your department
Your practice task is simple. Track a demo pump-test defect through assignment, repair, review, and documented return to service. Measure unresolved work-order age. Record the result, who owns the follow-up, and when it should be reviewed.

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Turn a response-vehicle or kit defect into a work order with an owner, state and completion evidence.
Search and rescue: Track defective radios or search equipment through review, repair and return to service.
Security companies and departments: Track patrol equipment and vehicle defects through assigned repairs and verification.
Wildland contractors: Track apparatus and tool defects with assignment impact and return-to-service evidence.
Industrial emergency response teams: Track response-equipment defects through repair ownership and verified completion.
