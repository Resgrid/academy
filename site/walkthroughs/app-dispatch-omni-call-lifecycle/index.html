Omni Dispatch: create, release, update and close a call

Save a pending call, dispatch a recipient, record a handoff and verify the closed status.

Audience: Dispatchers, Department administrators

Before you begin

  • Omni Dispatch and an account in a training department with permission for the screens shown.

Chapters

  1. Enter the call and confirm the location
  2. Save to the pending queue and dispatch
  3. Record the handoff and update the incident
  4. Close the call and confirm the saved status

Watch the walkthrough

Download video · Download captions

Step 1

Enter the call and confirm the location

In Omni Dispatch, open New Call for a community outreach standby. Enter a name your team will recognize and use Nature to describe what responders need to know. This exercise uses Medical and Routine. Choose the type and priority your department uses for this work. On the location section, select GPS and enter the latitude and longitude. Check the map against your intended site. A search team can use coordinates for a trail rendezvous, and an industrial response team can use them for an access gate.

Practice

Enter the call and confirm the location in a fictional training department.

Step 2

Save to the pending queue and dispatch

Use the contact picker if an existing department contact belongs on the call. Here we select Academy Community Partners. Check Save as pending and select Save Pending Call. The call now has a number in Pending Calls, where dispatch can find it again when it is ready for release. Open the pending call and start dispatch. In the recipient picker, select the intended recipient. Here we choose Wallace Boden. Check the selection count on Done before confirming. Back in the console, find the same call number under Active Calls and open Details.

Practice

Save to the pending queue and dispatch in a fictional training department.

Step 3

Record the handoff and update the incident

Select Notes and enter an update that will make sense to the next dispatcher. Save it, then verify that the entry appears on the call. A security desk could record when site staff completed a handoff, and a wildland contractor could record where the relief crew should report. Use Call actions and Edit Call to update the incident description. Verify the revised wording on the call.

Practice

Record the handoff and update the incident in a fictional training department.

Step 4

Close the call and confirm the saved status

When the exercise is over, choose Close Call, confirm the reason and enter a closeout note. Review Notify everyone who was dispatched before confirming. In this training exercise we leave it off. Back in the console, search for the call and verify that it has left the active queue. In this exercise we can also open History on a nearby call. Match the original call number and read its Closed status under Previous calls at this location.

Practice

Close the call and confirm the saved status in a fictional training department.
