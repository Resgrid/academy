Plan protected-data access and recovery
Welcome to Resgrid Academy. In this lesson, we will plan protected-data access and recovery. Sensitive content needs deliberate enrollment, controlled access, key recovery, and reviewed external disclosure paths. We will explain the features, the setup decisions, and a practical way to check the result.

A department scenario
A co-response team enrolls so client-related notes are protected in every module that stores them. Protects sensitive incident and personnel content, with controlled disclosure and key recovery. The examples in this series use a fictional department and are teaching scenarios, not default settings.

Data Protection
Data Protection. Protection enrollment, key recovery and access controls. Review the enrollment checklist and plan key recovery before enrolling. Availability also depends on ADP. Check access in the target department.

Protected workflows
Protected workflows. Send protected data to approved outside systems through reviewed workflows. Requires the Advanced Data Protection add-on. Enrollment is a separate step: plan two-factor sign-in and key recovery first, and review the effects on search, exports and integrations. Availability also depends on ADP, Enabled. Check access in the target department.

Advanced Data Protection
Advanced Data Protection. The Advanced Data Protection add-on: extra encryption and controlled access for sensitive data. Buying does not enroll the department. Review MFA, recovery, migration and integration behavior; this is not a compliance certification. Availability also depends on ADP. Check access in the target department.

Configure one useful workflow
For the training scenario, work in this order. Step 1. Confirm Advanced Data Protection access and review the enrollment checklist. Step 2. Prepare administrator MFA and department key-recovery arrangements. Step 3. Enroll through the owning screen and review supported content, search, exports, and integrations.

Verify the result
Now verify the result. Use sample protected content to verify an authorized read, a denied read, and the approved recovery procedure. Review egress separately. A common problem to watch for: Buying the add-on does not enroll the department. Enrollment and migration behavior must be confirmed for the target release.

Try it with your department
Your practice task is simple. Document who owns enrollment, key recovery, and disclosure review. Use only synthetic protected content for the recording. Record the result, who owns the follow-up, and when it should be reviewed.

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Review protected-record access, key and recovery prerequisites according to the service’s policy.
Search and rescue: Review protected operational details and recovery ownership; verify authorized access.
Security companies and departments: Review client/site protected-data access and recovery responsibilities.
Wildland contractors: Review protected assignment data and key/recovery ownership.
Industrial emergency response teams: Review protected facility information and recovery procedures with responsible owners.
