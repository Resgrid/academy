Trainings

Training assignments with materials and optional quizzes.

Why it helps
See who is qualified and whose certifications are expiring before it affects who can respond.

How to configure it
Create a training and assign it to the members or groups who need it.

Before you begin
Training module enabled

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Trainings: Assign approved response and outreach training; review acknowledgments and completion separately from competency or authorization.
Search and rescue: Trainings: Assign navigation, search and equipment training; follow up on incomplete sessions.
Security companies and departments: Trainings: Assign site procedures and incident-report training; review acknowledgment and completion before assigning duties.
Wildland contractors: Trainings: Assign seasonal crew and equipment training; review completion before mobilization.
Industrial emergency response teams: Trainings: Assign facility ERT, PPE and response-procedure training; track completion and follow-up.
