Maintenance policies and approvals

Repair approvals and safety holds.

Why it helps
Make authority and repair completion requirements explicit.

How to configure it
Decide who approves repairs and returns equipment to service.

Before you begin
Readiness Pro add-on access; Maintenance module enabled

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Maintenance policies and approvals: Turn a response-vehicle or kit defect into a work order with an owner, state and completion evidence.
Search and rescue: Maintenance policies and approvals: Track defective radios or search equipment through review, repair and return to service.
Security companies and departments: Maintenance policies and approvals: Track patrol equipment and vehicle defects through assigned repairs and verification.
Wildland contractors: Maintenance policies and approvals: Track apparatus and tool defects with assignment impact and return-to-service evidence.
Industrial emergency response teams: Maintenance policies and approvals: Track response-equipment defects through repair ownership and verified completion.
