Mapping

The live map of personnel, units and calls.

Why it helps
A current map shows where responders and apparatus are and puts important locations in front of dispatchers.

How to configure it
Set how long locations stay on the map in the mapping settings.

Before you begin
Mapping module enabled

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Mapping: Use current operational team positions for visit coverage; choose a location lifetime and follow the service’s location-access procedures.
Search and rescue: Mapping: Set a location lifetime suitable for remote search updates; use search-area POIs and distinguish stale positions from current team location.
Security companies and departments: Mapping: Map patrol vehicles and site POIs; review stale-location lifetime before treating an old position as current patrol coverage.
Wildland contractors: Mapping: Map staging, divisions and support vehicles; review device update frequency and stale location during remote assignments.
Industrial emergency response teams: Mapping: Map facility access points, response equipment and team positions; review indoor or weak-signal limitations and fallback procedures.
