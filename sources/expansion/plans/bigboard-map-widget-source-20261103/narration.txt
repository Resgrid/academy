A Big Board map can give everyone in a room the same view of calls, stations, and resources. This training board starts with several kinds of pins and labels. A search and rescue team might use it to watch a staging area. An industrial response team could focus the same widget on its facility.

Open Settings and choose Map. Under Map Options, Zoom Out to Show All Pins fits the view to the displayed pins. The Visible Pins switches control what the widget includes. Calls and linked calls have separate switches, so check both when choosing what the board needs to show.

Turn off Show Calls and leave stations, units, and personnel enabled. Save, then return to the map. The training call pins are gone while the other references remain. This is a display preference for the widget. It does not close any calls or remove assignments.

For a board that should stay centered on the same area, turn off Zoom Out to Show All Pins. Enter a zoom and set the center with latitude and longitude. We use a Chicago center. Keep the minus sign in the west longitude. Turn on Hide Labels to simplify the view.

After saving, check that the frame covers the area you intended. Our first zoom is too tight to show the resources. Reopen Map settings and set the zoom to twelve, then save again. The wider view keeps the fixed center while bringing the training pins back into the frame.

Now the pins remain but their Resgrid names are hidden. Street names from the basemap are still visible. Some training pins share a location, so their icons overlap. A community paramedicine team could use fixed framing for an outreach area, while a security team could keep the view centered on the sites it monitors. Check the actual board from its viewing distance after making changes.
