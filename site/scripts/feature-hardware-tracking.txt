Hardware location tracking

GPS devices that report unit locations.

Why it helps
Review device ownership, stale-position behavior and fallback sources.

How to configure it
Register each tracking device and link it to its unit.

Before you begin
Check your department access and intended audience.

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Hardware location tracking: Model response areas or outreach teams as groups and mobile response vehicles as units; define team positions appropriate to the service.
Search and rescue: Hardware location tracking: Model bases and field teams as groups and search vehicles or boats as units; define team leader and operator positions.
Security companies and departments: Hardware location tracking: Model clients or sites as groups and patrol vehicles as units; check membership before applying site-scoped dispatch.
Wildland contractors: Hardware location tracking: Model bases and crews as groups and engines or support vehicles as units; require the appropriate driver or crew positions.
Industrial emergency response teams: Hardware location tracking: Model facilities or plants as groups and response vehicles or equipment as units; define operators and response-team positions.
