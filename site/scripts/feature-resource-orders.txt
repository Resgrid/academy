Mutual-aid resource orders

Resource orders describe requested resources and the fills departments offer or accept.

Why it helps
Resource orders describe requested resources and the fills departments offer or accept.

How to configure it
Review ordering settings and participating departments. Create a fictional request, offer a fill, and review acceptance.

Before you begin
Check your department access and intended audience.

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Mutual-aid resource orders: Plan an outreach event or temporary response-coverage deployment with dates, roster and operational objectives.
Search and rescue: Mutual-aid resource orders: Plan a search deployment with staging, operational period, personnel and resource roster.
Security companies and departments: Mutual-aid resource orders: Plan event-security or temporary site coverage with an operational roster and dates.
Wildland contractors: Mutual-aid resource orders: Plan a crew assignment with operational dates and resources; review cost evidence separately from the operational roster.
Industrial emergency response teams: Mutual-aid resource orders: Plan plant turnaround or mutual-aid coverage with dates, response resources and responsible reviewers.
