Contact Categories

Categories for organizing contacts.

Why it helps
Members find the current procedure, preplan or phone number when they need it instead of searching email.

How to configure it
Gather the documents and contacts members need most, and decide who keeps them current.

Before you begin
Check your department access and intended audience.

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Contact Categories: Organize approved response and referral procedures with responsible owners and review dates.
Search and rescue: Contact Categories: Organize search, navigation and equipment protocols and mutual-aid references.
Security companies and departments: Contact Categories: Organize site instructions, escalation procedures and patrol reference documents.
Wildland contractors: Contact Categories: Organize assignment, equipment and agency procedures with seasonal review ownership.
Industrial emergency response teams: Contact Categories: Organize facility response plans, access instructions and equipment procedures with current owners.
