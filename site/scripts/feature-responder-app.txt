Responder application

The member app for receiving dispatches, responding and setting status.

Why it helps
Separate member device setup from department configuration and verify each member's channels.

How to configure it
Have every member install the Responder app, sign in and allow notifications.

Before you begin
Check your department access and intended audience.

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Responder application: Choose member, dispatch and display apps according to response-team duties; verify device registration and external providers separately.
Search and rescue: Responder application: Choose field-member, dispatch and coordination-display apps; verify location update and remote connectivity.
Security companies and departments: Responder application: Choose patrol-member, dispatcher and site-display apps; verify site scope and device registration.
Wildland contractors: Responder application: Choose crew-member, dispatch and coordination apps; verify device updates during remote assignments.
Industrial emergency response teams: Responder application: Choose responder, control-room and display apps; verify provider prerequisites and facility connectivity.
