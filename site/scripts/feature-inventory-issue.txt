Issue Equipment

Issue equipment to members and record returns.

Why it helps
Know what you have, where it is and what is about to expire.

How to configure it
Decide which supplies and equipment are worth tracking and where they are stored.

Before you begin
Inventory module enabled

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Issue Equipment: Track operational kits and consumables by location; record issues and returns without exposing clinical information.
Search and rescue: Issue Equipment: Track rope, radios and search kits; issue equipment to a team, then reconcile returns and damaged items.
Security companies and departments: Issue Equipment: Track radios, PPE and site keys as appropriate to inventory policy; reconcile equipment issued to patrol staff.
Wildland contractors: Issue Equipment: Track tools, hose and consumables by base or crew; record transfers and returns after an assignment.
Industrial emergency response teams: Issue Equipment: Track PPE, spill supplies and response kits; review stock, expiry and damaged equipment.
