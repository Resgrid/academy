Unit Staffing

Assign crew members to units.

Why it helps
Units let dispatchers send the right resources and let everyone see which apparatus is available.

How to configure it
List your apparatus or response teams with their types and home stations. Teams that respond only as individuals can mark this module not applicable.

Before you begin
Check your department access and intended audience.

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Unit Staffing: Model response areas or outreach teams as groups and mobile response vehicles as units; define team positions appropriate to the service.
Search and rescue: Unit Staffing: Model bases and field teams as groups and search vehicles or boats as units; define team leader and operator positions.
Security companies and departments: Unit Staffing: Model clients or sites as groups and patrol vehicles as units; check membership before applying site-scoped dispatch.
Wildland contractors: Unit Staffing: Model bases and crews as groups and engines or support vehicles as units; require the appropriate driver or crew positions.
Industrial emergency response teams: Unit Staffing: Model facilities or plants as groups and response vehicles or equipment as units; define operators and response-team positions.
