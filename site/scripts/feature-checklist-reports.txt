Checklist compliance reports

Completed, skipped and overdue checks over time.

Why it helps
Find missing recorded checks without treating a report as safety certification.

How to configure it
Pick the checks you do on paper today and who completes them.

Before you begin
Checklists module enabled

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Checklist compliance reports: Use a vehicle or operational-kit checklist; follow up on failed items and distinguish scheduled compliance from an on-demand check.
Search and rescue: Checklist compliance reports: Use search-kit and vehicle readiness checks; assign failed equipment follow-up.
Security companies and departments: Checklist compliance reports: Use patrol vehicle or site-opening checks; review failures and missed scheduled checks.
Wildland contractors: Checklist compliance reports: Use crew and apparatus readiness checks before mobilization; create accountable defect follow-up.
Industrial emergency response teams: Checklist compliance reports: Use response-kit and equipment checks; review required items and route failures to maintenance.
