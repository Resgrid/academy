Work Orders

Repair and maintenance work orders.

Why it helps
Turn a failed check or reported defect into a tracked repair and a documented return to service.

How to configure it
Create work orders from failed checks or reported defects.

Before you begin
Feature access enabled by your system operator; Maintenance module enabled; Readiness Pro add-on access

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Work Orders: Turn a response-vehicle or kit defect into a work order with an owner, state and completion evidence.
Search and rescue: Work Orders: Track defective radios or search equipment through review, repair and return to service.
Security companies and departments: Work Orders: Track patrol equipment and vehicle defects through assigned repairs and verification.
Wildland contractors: Work Orders: Track apparatus and tool defects with assignment impact and return-to-service evidence.
Industrial emergency response teams: Work Orders: Track response-equipment defects through repair ownership and verified completion.
