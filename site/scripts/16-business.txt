Connect service activity to costs and billing
Welcome to Resgrid Academy. In this lesson, we will connect service activity to costs and billing. Re-entering activity into estimates, invoices, and cost spreadsheets creates inconsistent rates and missed supporting evidence. We will explain the features, the setup decisions, and a practical way to check the result.

A department scenario
An event standby is invoiced from its deployment record, and a mutual-aid claim is prepared for MARS reimbursement. Bill for standby and contract services and prepare reimbursement claims from records you already keep. The examples in this series use a fictional department and are teaching scenarios, not default settings.

Configure one useful workflow
For the training scenario, work in this order. Step 1. Confirm Business Operations access and configure billing identity and approved rates. Step 2. Prepare a demo bid or contract and the corresponding service invoice. Step 3. Review payments, invoice aging, deployment costs, or MARS claim evidence as applicable.

Verify the result
Now verify the result. Reconcile source activity, quantities, rates, totals, and recorded payment or claim outcomes. Use a payment-provider test environment. A common problem to watch for: Pay-data reporting also requires enabled Advanced Data Protection. Annual rates and reporting requirements need current finance review.

Try it with your department
Your practice task is simple. Prepare a standby estimate and draft invoice from sample activity. Compare recorded cost with the billed amount and explain any difference. Record the result, who owns the follow-up, and when it should be reviewed.

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Connect approved operational activity to the service’s cost workflow; review eligibility and export prerequisites outside the operational roster.
Search and rescue: Keep operational search records and any approved cost evidence together without assuming reimbursement eligibility.
Security companies and departments: Connect client/site service activity to reviewed costs and invoices; verify source activity before authorized billing.
Wildland contractors: Connect crew assignment activity and approved costs to the contracting workflow; validate rates and evidence before financial submission.
Industrial emergency response teams: Connect facility or contracted response activity to reviewed internal cost allocation and invoicing where applicable.
