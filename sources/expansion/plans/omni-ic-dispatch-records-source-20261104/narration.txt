Open the intended call in Omni Dispatch, then select Records. This view shows the records linked to this call. Choose New Record to start a department form in the call context.

Choose the published Dispatch coordination note for this exercise. Your department may offer different forms. The form you choose determines which fields, permissions and review steps apply.

Enter a Summary and Notes. This fictional example records a briefing-location handoff. Search and rescue, community outreach and security dispatch desks can use the same approach for their department forms. Choose Save and send.

The server opens the saved record and marks it Draft. Verify the form and the saved values before going further. A saved draft can still be edited.

For this exercise, check the attestation after reviewing the information, then choose Finalize. Use the lifecycle steps required by your department for the form you are completing.

Check the permanent record number and the Finalized status. Those are the signals that this record completed its lifecycle.

Return to the call and open Records again. The same number and Finalized status appear in the list. That gives the next dispatcher a reliable way to find the handoff.
