Environment limits

The application containers use an internal network. Real notifications are suppressed, email addresses use .example, and SMTP points to Mailpit. Paid module access uses a read-only local billing fixture with zero charges. External SMS, voice, push, CAD, PTT, ADP, NERIS submission, payments, mobile apps, and Relay are not verified. AI and sensitive feature overrides remain off.

These videos and screenshots use fictional Firehouse 51 training data.
