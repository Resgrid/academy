Certification Settings

What happens when a certification expires, grace periods and reminders.

Why it helps
See who is qualified and whose certifications are expiring before it affects who can respond.

How to configure it
List the certifications your department tracks and their renewal periods.

Before you begin
Check your department access and intended audience.

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Certification Settings: Assign approved response and outreach training; review acknowledgments and completion separately from competency or authorization.
Search and rescue: Certification Settings: Assign navigation, search and equipment training; follow up on incomplete sessions.
Security companies and departments: Certification Settings: Assign site procedures and incident-report training; review acknowledgment and completion before assigning duties.
Wildland contractors: Certification Settings: Assign seasonal crew and equipment training; review completion before mobilization.
Industrial emergency response teams: Certification Settings: Assign facility ERT, PPE and response-procedure training; track completion and follow-up.
