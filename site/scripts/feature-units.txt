Units

Your apparatus, vehicles and response teams.

Why it helps
Units let dispatchers send the right resources and let everyone see which apparatus is available.

How to configure it
Add each unit with its type and home station group.

Before you begin
Check your department access and intended audience.

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Units: Model response areas or outreach teams as groups and mobile response vehicles as units; define team positions appropriate to the service.
Search and rescue: Units: Model bases and field teams as groups and search vehicles or boats as units; define team leader and operator positions.
Security companies and departments: Units: Model clients or sites as groups and patrol vehicles as units; check membership before applying site-scoped dispatch.
Wildland contractors: Units: Model bases and crews as groups and engines or support vehicles as units; require the appropriate driver or crew positions.
Industrial emergency response teams: Units: Model facilities or plants as groups and response vehicles or equipment as units; define operators and response-team positions.
