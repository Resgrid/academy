Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: New Training: Assign approved response and outreach training; review acknowledgments and completion separately from competency or authorization.
Search and rescue: New Training: Assign navigation, search and equipment training; follow up on incomplete sessions.
Security companies and departments: New Training: Assign site procedures and incident-report training; review acknowledgment and completion before assigning duties.
Wildland contractors: New Training: Assign seasonal crew and equipment training; review completion before mobilization.
Industrial emergency response teams: New Training: Assign facility ERT, PPE and response-procedure training; track completion and follow-up.
