Import and migration planning

Plan moving your data from another system into Resgrid.

Why it helps
Avoid duplicate resources and verify resulting configuration in Setup Report.

How to configure it
Have the destination systems and credentials ready, and decide who owns each automation.

Before you begin
Check your department access and intended audience.

Examples for your department
