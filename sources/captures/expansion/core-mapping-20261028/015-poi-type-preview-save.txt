Icon guidelines
PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP, up to 1 MB. SVG is not supported, and an animated GIF uses its first frame.
At least 16 px and at most 4096 px on each edge. A square image of 128 × 128 px or larger looks sharpest.
Use a transparent background and a bold, simple shape. Maps show the icon at 32 × 32 px and lists at 20 × 20 px, so fine detail and text will not be readable.
Every icon is resized to 128 × 128 px and saved as PNG. An image that is not square is centered on a transparent square, never cropped.
