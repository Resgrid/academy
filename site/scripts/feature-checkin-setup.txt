Check-in timers

Timers that remind responders on a call to check in, with escalation when they do not.

Why it helps
Review lone-worker and response follow-up with approved local procedures.

How to configure it
Collect your call types, the statuses members report while responding, and the details of any CAD or paging system that should create calls.

Before you begin
Check your department access and intended audience.

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Check-in timers: Use an approved operational request type for a visit or mobile response; keep clinical triage and sensitive detail under the service’s procedures.
Search and rescue: Check-in timers: Use Rescue or a reviewed search type and clear staging information; select the appropriate team.
Security companies and departments: Check-in timers: Use Investigation or a reviewed patrol incident type with the correct site and selected resources.
Wildland contractors: Check-in timers: Use a reviewed assignment or incident type and staging details; select crew and support resources.
Industrial emergency response teams: Check-in timers: Use a reviewed facility incident type and access location; select the facility team and specialist equipment.
