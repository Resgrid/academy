Indoor maps

Floor plans and zones for buildings.

Why it helps
Keep building references available to authorized users without inferring safe routes.

How to configure it
Confirm members allow location sharing in the Responder app, and decide how long a position should stay on the map.

Before you begin
Mapping module enabled

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Indoor maps: Use current operational team positions for visit coverage; choose a location lifetime and follow the service’s location-access procedures.
Search and rescue: Indoor maps: Set a location lifetime suitable for remote search updates; use search-area POIs and distinguish stale positions from current team location.
Security companies and departments: Indoor maps: Map patrol vehicles and site POIs; review stale-location lifetime before treating an old position as current patrol coverage.
Wildland contractors: Indoor maps: Map staging, divisions and support vehicles; review device update frequency and stale location during remote assignments.
Industrial emergency response teams: Indoor maps: Map facility access points, response equipment and team positions; review indoor or weak-signal limitations and fallback procedures.
