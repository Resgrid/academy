Incident command and accountability

Incident command and accountability for incident commanders.

Why it helps
Prepare command structures and access before an exercise or response.

How to configure it
Give command access to the officers who run incidents.

Before you begin
Command App Login permission

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Incident command and accountability: Choose member, dispatch and display apps according to response-team duties; verify device registration and external providers separately.
Search and rescue: Incident command and accountability: Choose field-member, dispatch and coordination-display apps; verify location update and remote connectivity.
Security companies and departments: Incident command and accountability: Choose patrol-member, dispatcher and site-display apps; verify site scope and device registration.
Wildland contractors: Incident command and accountability: Choose crew-member, dispatch and coordination apps; verify device updates during remote assignments.
Industrial emergency response teams: Incident command and accountability: Choose responder, control-room and display apps; verify provider prerequisites and facility connectivity.
