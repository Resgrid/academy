Unit types

Categories such as Engine, Ladder or Ambulance.

Why it helps
Group comparable resources without assuming staffing or qualification.

How to configure it
Create unit types before adding units so each unit can be classified.

Before you begin
Check your department access and intended audience.

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Unit types: Model response areas or outreach teams as groups and mobile response vehicles as units; define team positions appropriate to the service.
Search and rescue: Unit types: Model bases and field teams as groups and search vehicles or boats as units; define team leader and operator positions.
Security companies and departments: Unit types: Model clients or sites as groups and patrol vehicles as units; check membership before applying site-scoped dispatch.
Wildland contractors: Unit types: Model bases and crews as groups and engines or support vehicles as units; require the appropriate driver or crew positions.
Industrial emergency response teams: Unit types: Model facilities or plants as groups and response vehicles or equipment as units; define operators and response-team positions.
