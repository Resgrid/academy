Workforce

Workforce compensation and field cost runs.

Why it helps
Bill for standby and contract services and prepare reimbursement claims from records you already keep.

How to configure it
Add compensation profiles, then run field costs for deployments.

Before you begin
Feature access enabled by your system operator; Business Operations module enabled; Business Operations add-on access

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Workforce: Connect approved operational activity to the service’s cost workflow; review eligibility and export prerequisites outside the operational roster.
Search and rescue: Workforce: Keep operational search records and any approved cost evidence together without assuming reimbursement eligibility.
Security companies and departments: Workforce: Connect client/site service activity to reviewed costs and invoices; verify source activity before authorized billing.
Wildland contractors: Workforce: Connect crew assignment activity and approved costs to the contracting workflow; validate rates and evidence before financial submission.
Industrial emergency response teams: Workforce: Connect facility or contracted response activity to reviewed internal cost allocation and invoicing where applicable.
