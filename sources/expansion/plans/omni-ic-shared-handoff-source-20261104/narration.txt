Before your shift, check the Shared device label on the Omni IC sign-in screen. Here it says Academy IC handoff desk. A named desk helps staff recognize the device they are using. Sign in with your own account for your shift.

Open Sign-in and security in Settings to check your verification methods. This example has no authenticator app or passkeys enrolled. If your department uses verification for shared devices, confirm your setup before taking over a desk.

When you step away, find Session in Settings and choose Lock. A desk shared by an industrial response team or security shift can use the same control.

The lock screen shows the signed-in operator. This training shift cannot be unlocked here because the account has no eligible factor. Use End shift to return to sign-in. Use Switch operator when another authorized operator is taking over.

After End shift, the sign-in form returns with the same device label. The next operator can start a shift with their own account. This is useful at a search command post or a community outreach coordination desk.
