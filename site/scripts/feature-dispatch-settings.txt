Dispatch Settings
Production status: source-backed recording brief; capture pending
Problem / impact: Consistent call types and statuses make dispatching, response tracking and reporting reliable from the first call.
What it does: How calls page personnel: by group or by shift, and related automatic status changes.
Prerequisites: []
Release: available

CAPTURE SCRIPT
1. Open Dispatch Settings as the intended dispatchers and administrators.
2. Choose whether dispatch follows groups or shifts, and review the automatic status options.
3. Demonstrate one controlled sample through the owning screen.
4. Check the saved call details and intended recipients. Use a controlled communication test to evaluate delivery separately from the saved dispatch.
5. Show one common issue and the evidence used to diagnose it.

ADAPT THIS WORKFLOW TO YOUR DEPARTMENT
Firehouse 51 is synthetic demonstration data. Apply the same workflow to your department’s procedures.

Community paramedicine and mental health response: Dispatch Settings: Use an approved operational request type for a visit or mobile response; keep clinical triage and sensitive detail under the service’s procedures.

Search and rescue: Dispatch Settings: Use Rescue or a reviewed search type and clear staging information; select the appropriate team.

Security companies and departments: Dispatch Settings: Use Investigation or a reviewed patrol incident type with the correct site and selected resources.

Wildland contractors: Dispatch Settings: Use a reviewed assignment or incident type and staging details; select crew and support resources.

Industrial emergency response teams: Dispatch Settings: Use a reviewed facility incident type and access location; select the facility team and specialist equipment.
