Give members a dependable reference library
Welcome to Resgrid Academy. In this lesson, we will give members a dependable reference library. Procedures and contacts hidden in individual email threads are difficult to find and keep current. We will explain the features, the setup decisions, and a practical way to check the result.

A department scenario
A department uploads its standard operating procedures, adds hospital and mutual-aid contacts and creates a medical dispatch protocol. Members find the current procedure, preplan or phone number when they need it instead of searching email. The examples in this series use a fictional department and are teaching scenarios, not default settings.

Configure one useful workflow
For the training scenario, work in this order. Step 1. Add key outside contacts and organize categories. Step 2. Upload a current procedure and set its intended visibility. Step 3. Create approved protocol questions or a form for a repeatable dispatch task.

Verify the result
Now verify the result. Open the contact, document, protocol, and form as an intended member. Check ownership, version currency, attachments, and visibility. A common problem to watch for: A shared link needs an explicit data-sharing review. Keep protocol content aligned with department-approved procedures.

Try it with your department
Your practice task is simple. Find the hospital contact and current procedure as a sample member. Track outdated material and items without a responsible owner. Record the result, who owns the follow-up, and when it should be reviewed.

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Organize approved response and referral procedures with responsible owners and review dates.
Search and rescue: Organize search, navigation and equipment protocols and mutual-aid references.
Security companies and departments: Organize site instructions, escalation procedures and patrol reference documents.
Wildland contractors: Organize assignment, equipment and agency procedures with seasonal review ownership.
Industrial emergency response teams: Organize facility response plans, access instructions and equipment procedures with current owners.
