Weather zones and alerts

Weather alert zones and alert notifications.

Why it helps
Review alert coverage and automatic-message settings before use.

How to configure it
Draw the zones you cover and choose who is alerted.

Before you begin
Check your department access and intended audience.

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Weather zones and alerts: Use current operational team positions for visit coverage; choose a location lifetime and follow the service’s location-access procedures.
Search and rescue: Weather zones and alerts: Set a location lifetime suitable for remote search updates; use search-area POIs and distinguish stale positions from current team location.
Security companies and departments: Weather zones and alerts: Map patrol vehicles and site POIs; review stale-location lifetime before treating an old position as current patrol coverage.
Wildland contractors: Weather zones and alerts: Map staging, divisions and support vehicles; review device update frequency and stale location during remote assignments.
Industrial emergency response teams: Weather zones and alerts: Map facility access points, response equipment and team positions; review indoor or weak-signal limitations and fallback procedures.
