Permissions

Who can create calls, see personal information and perform other sensitive actions.

Why it helps
Review who can perform sensitive work and which resources they may see.

How to configure it
Review each permission; some default to every member.

Before you begin
Check your department access and intended audience.

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Permissions: Set the local time zone for scheduled outreach visits; review whether a daily reset would incorrectly mark an occupied response team available.
Search and rescue: Permissions: Choose availability reset rules that suit volunteers and overnight searches; avoid implying that a remote volunteer has arrived at staging.
Security companies and departments: Permissions: Set patrol-day boundaries and display order; review staffing suppression before excluding an off-duty patrol member from alerts.
Wildland contractors: Permissions: Review reset times across overnight assignments and the deployment time zone before changing contractor crew availability.
Industrial emergency response teams: Permissions: Align display time with plant shifts and review automatic resets before overwriting a team engaged in an extended incident.
