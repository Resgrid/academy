Assign learning and track qualifications
Welcome to Resgrid Academy. In this lesson, we will assign learning and track qualifications. Scattered training records make it harder to see incomplete assignments and upcoming certification renewals. We will explain the features, the setup decisions, and a practical way to check the result.

A department scenario
EMT and Driver/Operator certification types are added with their renewal periods, and an annual refresher training is assigned to every member. See who is qualified and whose certifications are expiring before it affects who can respond. The examples in this series use a fictional department and are teaching scenarios, not default settings.

Trainings
Trainings. Training assignments with materials and optional quizzes. Create a training and assign it to the members or groups who need it. Availability also depends on Training. Check access in the target department.

Certification Types
Certification Types. The certifications and qualifications you track. Add each certification type with its renewal period.

Certification Dashboard
Certification Dashboard. Who holds which certifications and what is expiring. Record members' current certifications, then review what expires soon.

New Training
New Training. Create a training, with an optional quiz. List the certifications your department tracks and their renewal periods. Availability also depends on Training. Check access in the target department.

Certification Settings
Certification Settings. What happens when a certification expires, grace periods and reminders. List the certifications your department tracks and their renewal periods.

My Certifications
My Certifications. Your own certification records. List the certifications your department tracks and their renewal periods.

Configure one useful workflow
For the training scenario, work in this order. Step 1. Create certification types with their renewal periods. Step 2. Record current member certifications and review expiry settings. Step 3. Create a training with materials and an optional quiz, then assign the intended audience.

Verify the result
Now verify the result. Check a sample member completion and certification expiry. Review reminders, grace periods, and any configured enforcement effects. A common problem to watch for: A completed training, a personnel role, and a current certification represent different facts. Track each through its owning feature.

Try it with your department
Your practice task is simple. Assign a refresher to the demo group and complete it as a member. Review overdue learning and certifications approaching renewal. Record the result, who owns the follow-up, and when it should be reviewed.

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Assign approved response and outreach training; review acknowledgments and completion separately from competency or authorization.
Search and rescue: Assign navigation, search and equipment training; follow up on incomplete sessions.
Security companies and departments: Assign site procedures and incident-report training; review acknowledgment and completion before assigning duties.
Wildland contractors: Assign seasonal crew and equipment training; review completion before mobilization.
Industrial emergency response teams: Assign facility ERT, PPE and response-procedure training; track completion and follow-up.
