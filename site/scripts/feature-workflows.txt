Workflows

Automations triggered by department events.

Why it helps
Automate repeated administrative steps and connect Resgrid to the other systems you use.

How to configure it
Start with one workflow for a task you repeat, and review its runs.

Before you begin
Check your department access and intended audience.

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Workflows: Use a reviewed workflow for an operational handover task; inspect external provider output before adoption.
Search and rescue: Workflows: Use a reviewed workflow for search logistics or follow-up; inspect failed executions.
Security companies and departments: Workflows: Use a reviewed workflow for patrol follow-up; verify client/site scope and external delivery.
Wildland contractors: Workflows: Use a reviewed workflow for assignment follow-up; inspect provider failures and retries.
Industrial emergency response teams: Workflows: Use a reviewed workflow for drill or equipment follow-up; inspect execution history.
