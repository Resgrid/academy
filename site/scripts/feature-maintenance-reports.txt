Maintenance history and reports

Maintenance history, repair costs and safety holds.

Why it helps
Support accountable return-to-service review through the owning workflow.

How to configure it
Requires the Readiness Pro add-on. Checklists remain available without it.

Before you begin
Readiness Pro add-on access; Maintenance module enabled

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Maintenance history and reports: Turn a response-vehicle or kit defect into a work order with an owner, state and completion evidence.
Search and rescue: Maintenance history and reports: Track defective radios or search equipment through review, repair and return to service.
Security companies and departments: Maintenance history and reports: Track patrol equipment and vehicle defects through assigned repairs and verification.
Wildland contractors: Maintenance history and reports: Track apparatus and tool defects with assignment impact and return-to-service evidence.
Industrial emergency response teams: Maintenance history and reports: Track response-equipment defects through repair ownership and verified completion.
