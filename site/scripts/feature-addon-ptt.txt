Push-to-Talk

The Push-to-Talk add-on: voice channels in the Resgrid apps.

Why it helps
Coordinate field teams through configured voice channels.

How to configure it
Review seats, supported clients and channel setup. PTT does not provide phone dispatch alerts or certify radio replacement.

Before you begin
Push-to-Talk add-on access

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Push-to-Talk: Plan operational team channels and verify device and add-on prerequisites.
Search and rescue: Push-to-Talk: Plan search-team and staging channels; test approved participants and fallback radio procedures.
Security companies and departments: Push-to-Talk: Plan site and patrol channels with intended memberships; test authorized device access.
Wildland contractors: Push-to-Talk: Plan crew and staging channels; test connectivity and approved fallback communications.
Industrial emergency response teams: Push-to-Talk: Plan facility response channels; test device access and approved fallback communications.
