Record revisions and differences

Revision and difference views help reviewers understand how a record changed.

Why it helps
Revision and difference views help reviewers understand how a record changed.

How to configure it
Open two revisions of a synthetic record and review their differences and any permitted export.

Before you begin
Check your department access and intended audience.

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Record revisions and differences: Use an approved operational record definition for visits or responses; select fields and access according to the service’s record policy.
Search and rescue: Record revisions and differences: Record search activity, participants and outcomes; distinguish a saved draft from a reviewed or finalized record.
Security companies and departments: Record revisions and differences: Record patrol or incident activity under an appropriate definition; review required fields and client/site access.
Wildland contractors: Record revisions and differences: Record assignment activity and crew evidence; link the correct call or deployment and retain review status.
Industrial emergency response teams: Record revisions and differences: Record drills and response activity; review the definition, participants and finalization requirements.
