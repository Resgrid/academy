Unit · Video walkthrough

Complete route stops and verify the ended execution

Use manual stop check-ins and checkouts, end a route, and verify its saved completion and stop times in Core.

Audience: Unit crews, Logistics coordinators, Department administrators

Before you begin

  • A fictional practice department and permission to update the assigned unit’s route.
  • An active execution with reviewed stop locations. Core supervision requires appropriate access.

Chapters

  1. Keep manual progress separate from location
  2. Open the current stop and check in
  3. Check out and verify the next pending stop
  4. Complete the second stop
  5. End the execution deliberately
  6. Verify the saved execution in Core
  7. Read the retained stop times
  8. Compare history and apply the workflow

Watch the walkthrough

Download video · Download captions

Step 1

Keep manual progress separate from location

This exercise follows a fictional route already started for the assigned training unit. In Settings, Realtime Geolocation and Background Geolocation are off for this software exercise. The check-ins you will see are manual status updates. They do not demonstrate physical arrival or geofence validation. Open Routes and find the Active Route card. Its zero-percent progress is separate from the saved plan listed below it.

Practice

Keep manual progress separate from location

Step 2

Open the current stop and check in

Select Active Route. Current Step identifies Supply point A and shows Pending. Review the intended stop before selecting Check In. The returned current step now says In Progress, and Check Out replaces Check In. Progress remains zero percent because the stop is still in progress. In operational use, follow your department’s arrival procedure and confirm you are updating the correct assigned route.

Practice

Open the current stop and check in

Step 3

Check out and verify the next pending stop

After the first stop’s work is finished, select Check Out. The progress display changes to fifty percent. Current Step now identifies Community point B, with Pending and its Check In button. Check the name and state together. The changed current step and percentage provide immediate feedback that the first stop’s checkout was saved.

Practice

Check out and verify the next pending stop

Step 4

Complete the second stop

For Community point B, select Check In. The returned current step now says In Progress. Select Check Out after completing that stop’s work. The page now shows one hundred percent and Stops Completed. Scroll the stop list to reach End Route. Finishing all the stop checkouts and ending the route are separate actions; the End Route control is still available here.

Practice

Complete the second stop

Step 5

End the execution deliberately

Select End Route. Read the confirmation asking whether you want to end this route. Cancel leaves the execution open; confirm End Route when the assignment is finished. After confirmation, the app returns to Routes. The saved Community supply loop plan remains in the list, while the Active Route card is gone. Ending this execution has not deleted the reusable plan.

Practice

End the execution deliberately

Step 6

Verify the saved execution in Core

An authorized supervisor can reopen the same execution in Core. Instance Info now shows Completed, unit fifty-one thousand eight, a Started time, an Ended time, and progress of two out of two. Use these stored details to verify the result independently of the Unit screen’s navigation. The map here is not evidence of a recorded vehicle journey.

Practice

Verify the saved execution in Core

Step 7

Read the retained stop times

Scroll to Stop Timeline. Each of the two recorded entries shows Completed, an In time, an Out time, and Dwell. These are the saved times from the manual training check-ins and checkouts. Very short exercise dwell times are not operational travel estimates. Review these entries when investigating an execution, while keeping your department’s location and reporting procedures in mind.

Practice

Read the retained stop times

Step 8

Compare history and apply the workflow

Route History retains both completed practice executions. Compare Started and Ended to identify the intended run, then use Detail to reopen it. Both rows have two out of two stops completed. This workflow can support community paramedicine visits, mental health response logistics, search-and-rescue supply runs, security rounds, wildland support and industrial response teams, as well as fire and E M S. Choose meaningful stops, assign the right unit and verify saved results.
