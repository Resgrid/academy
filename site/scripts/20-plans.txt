Match subscription capacity to the roster
Welcome to Resgrid Academy. In this lesson, we will match subscription capacity to the roster. Large onboarding can stop at personnel or unit limits when capacity has not been reviewed in advance. We will explain the features, the setup decisions, and a practical way to check the result.

A department scenario
Before inviting 40 new members, an administrator confirms the plan has room or asks the managing member to change it. Adding members or units stops at the plan limit, so check the remaining room before a large onboarding. The examples in this series use a fictional department and are teaching scenarios, not default settings.

Base plans and capacity
Base plans and capacity. Your plan, its personnel and unit limits, and your add-ons. Check the remaining room before adding many members or units.

Configure one useful workflow
For the training scenario, work in this order. Step 1. Review the observed base plan, personnel count, and unit count. Step 2. Compare planned onboarding with available capacity. Step 3. Review add-on entitlements and their separate configuration prerequisites.

Verify the result
Now verify the result. Check observed subscription evidence and current resource counts. Use the owning billing screen for any authorized subscription change. A common problem to watch for: Missing billing evidence means capacity is unknown. Only the managing member performs subscription changes.

Try it with your department
Your practice task is simple. Compare current counts with a proposed roster expansion. Document headroom and optional add-ons without purchasing anything. Record the result, who owns the follow-up, and when it should be reviewed.

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Compare active member counts and enabled modules with subscription capacity; identify the owner of subscription changes.
Search and rescue: Plan seasonal volunteer capacity and relevant add-ons before increasing the roster.
Security companies and departments: Compare patrol-company roster growth and add-on needs with subscription capacity; review purchases with the account owner.
Wildland contractors: Review seasonal contractor roster and required add-ons; distinguish subscription billing from deployment cost recovery.
Industrial emergency response teams: Review facility roster and operational add-ons against subscription capacity; confirm external billing-system status.
