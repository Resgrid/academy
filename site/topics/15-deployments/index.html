Keep deployment activity and cost evidence together

Welcome to Resgrid Academy. In this lesson, we will keep deployment activity and cost evidence together. Extended mutual-aid work needs clear personnel, apparatus, dates, and cost records that can be reviewed after demobilization. We will explain the features, the setup decisions, and a practical way to check the result.

A department scenario

A strike-team deployment is recorded with its personnel, apparatus and dates. Keep deployment records and cost documentation together for review and reimbursement. The examples in this series use a fictional department and are teaching scenarios, not default settings.

Mutual-aid resource orders

Resource orders describe requested resources and the fills departments offer or accept. Review ordering settings and participating departments. Create a fictional request, offer a fill, and review acceptance. Reconcile requested quantities, accepted fills, and the remaining resource need. Confirm availability in the demo department before recording the live workflow.

External orders and deployment closeout

An external order records the department fills and their mobilization, release, and actual return home. Enter an approved sample external order, link its deployment, record the fill transitions, and complete closeout. Review dates and resource identity. This workspace records manual entry and artifact snapshots; it is not an external ordering connector. Confirm availability in the demo department before recording the live workflow.

Deployment time and export reports

Read-only deployment reports bring time entries, supporting artifacts, and export outputs together. Open a recorded demo deployment and review its time report, summary, and available exports. Reconcile each time entry with the roster and recorded dates before finance review. Confirm availability in the demo department before recording the live workflow.

Configure one useful workflow

For the training scenario, work in this order. Step 1. Create a deployment or start from an external resource order. Step 2. Identify the assigned personnel, apparatus, and operational dates. Step 3. Record deployment activity and finance documentation for review.

Verify the result

Now verify the result. Reconcile the deployment resources and dates with supporting records. Review any daily time and cost documentation required by the workflow. A common problem to watch for: Deployment Finance is separate from the Business Operations add-on. A prepared record does not guarantee reimbursement.

Try it with your department

Your practice task is simple. Record a two-day demo strike-team deployment and reconcile the participating people and units with the supporting documents. Record the result, who owns the follow-up, and when it should be reviewed.
