New Work Order

Open a work order.

Why it helps
Turn a failed check or reported defect into a tracked repair and a documented return to service.

How to configure it
Requires the Readiness Pro add-on. Checklists remain available without it.

Before you begin
Feature access enabled by your system operator; Maintenance module enabled; Readiness Pro add-on access

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: New Work Order: Turn a response-vehicle or kit defect into a work order with an owner, state and completion evidence.
Search and rescue: New Work Order: Track defective radios or search equipment through review, repair and return to service.
Security companies and departments: New Work Order: Track patrol equipment and vehicle defects through assigned repairs and verification.
Wildland contractors: New Work Order: Track apparatus and tool defects with assignment impact and return-to-service evidence.
Industrial emergency response teams: New Work Order: Track response-equipment defects through repair ownership and verified completion.

Documentation: https://docs.resgrid.com/web-app/work-orders/
