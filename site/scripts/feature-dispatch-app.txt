Dispatch application

The dispatcher view for creating and managing calls.

Why it helps
Give dispatch personnel a focused operating surface alongside web administration.

How to configure it
Give dispatch access to the people who dispatch, then have them sign in.

Before you begin
Dispatch App Login permission

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Dispatch application: Choose member, dispatch and display apps according to response-team duties; verify device registration and external providers separately.
Search and rescue: Dispatch application: Choose field-member, dispatch and coordination-display apps; verify location update and remote connectivity.
Security companies and departments: Dispatch application: Choose patrol-member, dispatcher and site-display apps; verify site scope and device registration.
Wildland contractors: Dispatch application: Choose crew-member, dispatch and coordination apps; verify device updates during remote assignments.
Industrial emergency response teams: Dispatch application: Choose responder, control-room and display apps; verify provider prerequisites and facility connectivity.
