ADAPT THIS WORKFLOW TO YOUR DEPARTMENT
Firehouse 51 is synthetic demonstration data. Apply the same workflow to your department’s procedures.

Community paramedicine and mental health response: Call templates: Use an approved operational request type for a visit or mobile response; keep clinical triage and sensitive detail under the service’s procedures.

Search and rescue: Call templates: Use Rescue or a reviewed search type and clear staging information; select the appropriate team.

Security companies and departments: Call templates: Use Investigation or a reviewed patrol incident type with the correct site and selected resources.

Wildland contractors: Call templates: Use a reviewed assignment or incident type and staging details; select crew and support resources.

Industrial emergency response teams: Call templates: Use a reviewed facility incident type and access location; select the facility team and specialist equipment.

Practice: reproduce the configuration in your training department, save it, inspect the result, and identify the responsible reviewer. Sector examples are written companions; their presence does not certify provider delivery, clinical scope, or an updated video export.
