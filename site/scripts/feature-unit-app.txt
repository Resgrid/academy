Unit application

The in-vehicle app that represents an apparatus.

Why it helps
Review device identity, assigned crew and unit communications together.

How to configure it
Install the Unit app on each apparatus tablet and select its unit.

Before you begin
Check your department access and intended audience.

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Unit application: Choose member, dispatch and display apps according to response-team duties; verify device registration and external providers separately.
Search and rescue: Unit application: Choose field-member, dispatch and coordination-display apps; verify location update and remote connectivity.
Security companies and departments: Unit application: Choose patrol-member, dispatcher and site-display apps; verify site scope and device registration.
Wildland contractors: Unit application: Choose crew-member, dispatch and coordination apps; verify device updates during remote assignments.
Industrial emergency response teams: Unit application: Choose responder, control-room and display apps; verify provider prerequisites and facility connectivity.
