Checklist Templates

Ready-made checklists for common checks.

Why it helps
Recorded checks show what was inspected and what was missed.

How to configure it
Start from a template or build a checklist for each apparatus.

Before you begin
Feature access enabled by your system operator

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Checklist Templates: Use a vehicle or operational-kit checklist; follow up on failed items and distinguish scheduled compliance from an on-demand check.
Search and rescue: Checklist Templates: Use search-kit and vehicle readiness checks; assign failed equipment follow-up.
Security companies and departments: Checklist Templates: Use patrol vehicle or site-opening checks; review failures and missed scheduled checks.
Wildland contractors: Checklist Templates: Use crew and apparatus readiness checks before mobilization; create accountable defect follow-up.
Industrial emergency response teams: Checklist Templates: Use response-kit and equipment checks; review required items and route failures to maintenance.
