Call demand
Call hot zones
Heat map of every call in the last 12 months. The brightest areas are where calls cluster.
Busiest areas (grid)
A hexagon grid that counts the calls in each area, so you can compare neighborhoods by number instead of by glow.
Calls by type
Each call in the last 90 days as a dot, colored by call type.
Main call type by area
A grid colored by the most common call type in each area. Stronger color means more calls.
Calls by priority
Calls in the last 90 days colored by priority, to see where urgent calls come from.
Call outcomes
Calls colored by how they were closed: closed, cancelled, unfounded, false alarm, transferred and more.
Time of day
Calls colored by when they came in: overnight, morning, afternoon or evening. Useful for staffing and shift planning.
Weekday vs weekend
Calls split into weekday and weekend, for planning volunteer availability.
Seasonal patterns
Two years of calls colored by month, to spot seasonal demand such as wildland, water rescue or event seasons.
Response performance
Response time by area (90th percentile)
A grid colored by the 90th-percentile time from call logged to first unit on scene, the way NFPA 1710 and 1720 goals are measured. Needs units to set on-scene statuses.
Response time bands
Each call colored by minutes to the first unit on scene: under 4, 4 to 8, 8 to 12, 12 to 20 and over 20.
Time to first responding by area
A grid colored by the median time from call logged to the first unit responding or en route.
Time committed by area
A grid colored by the average time from call logged to call closed: where calls tie up resources the longest.
Coverage
Calls outside station coverage
Calls colored by straight-line distance to the nearest station, with 1.5, 2.5 and 5 mile rings (common insurance rating benchmarks).
Workload by station area
Calls colored by their home station (its geofence first, then who was dispatched), to compare first-due workload.
Where responders come from
Heat map of where members were when they marked themselves responding, with a 5 mile station ring. Needs location sharing in the app.
Units and personnel
Where units go
Every unit response in the last 90 days at the call location, colored by unit.
Demand by unit type
A grid colored by the unit type that responds most in each area, such as engines, ambulances or rescues.
Personnel response footprint
Heat map of the calls members responded to. Calls with more responders glow brighter.
Community risk
Repeat locations
Locations with 3 or more calls in the last 12 months, sized and colored by how many. Useful for frequent callers, alarm problems and prevention follow-up.
False alarm hot spots
Heat map of calls closed as false alarm or unfounded.
Tracking
Unit movement (last 24 hours)
Recorded unit paths for the last day, colored by unit.
Incident footprint
Pick a call to see where every unit and member went while working it: their paths, status positions and where responders came from.
Choose a call on the Customize tab.
Patrol coverage
Heat map of unit positions over the last 7 days: where patrols and field units spend their time.
Search coverage
A grid of member positions plus member and unit paths over the last 3 days, to see which areas have been covered.
