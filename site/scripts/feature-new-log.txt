1. Open New Log as the intended report writers, reviewers, and records officers.

ADAPT THIS WORKFLOW TO YOUR DEPARTMENT
Firehouse 51 is synthetic demonstration data. Apply the same workflow to your department’s procedures.

Community paramedicine and mental health response: New Log: Use an approved operational record definition for visits or responses; select fields and access according to the service’s record policy.

Search and rescue: New Log: Record search activity, participants and outcomes; distinguish a saved draft from a reviewed or finalized record.

Security companies and departments: New Log: Record patrol or incident activity under an appropriate definition; review required fields and client/site access.

Wildland contractors: New Log: Record assignment activity and crew evidence; link the correct call or deployment and retain review status.

Industrial emergency response teams: New Log: Record drills and response activity; review the definition, participants and finalization requirements.

Practice: reproduce the configuration in your training department, save it, inspect the result, and identify the responsible reviewer. Sector examples are written companions; their presence does not certify provider delivery, clinical scope, or an updated video export.
