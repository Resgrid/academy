Start in Core on the Calls dashboard. Open Archived Calls, then choose Add Archived Call. This is where you can enter an incident that has already happened.

Give the call a name that will make it easy to find later. This exercise is a completed station inspection.

Open the Timestamp picker. Choose the date of the original incident in the department’s time zone.

Pick the original time. Here, the inspection was on November fourth at two fifteen in the afternoon. Check the timestamp before moving on, and choose the call type and priority.

Use Nature of the Call to explain what happened. Keep the description brief and specific, so the next dispatcher understands why the record exists.

Enter the location. If you have coordinates, check the latitude and longitude before you save. This is a fictional training site.

At the bottom, choose the completion type and enter the closeout notes. For this exercise, leave Regenerate Call Numbers unchecked. Choose Add Archived Call to save.

Search the archive for the name you entered. Confirm the status is Closed and the timestamp matches the original time.

Open the record to verify the details. Logged On is the historical incident time. Closed On shows when the archive entry was completed.

Now open Archived Calls in Omni Dispatch.

Search for the same name. Check that Logged On still says two fifteen in the afternoon, and that Closed On matches the closeout in Core. Use the same process to document a completed security patrol or a search and rescue assignment that needs to be logged after the fact.
