Unit: read floor plans and find saved rooms

Open an indoor reference map, inspect saved room details, switch layers and filter imported regions.

Audience: Unit crews, Site and logistics coordinators, Department administrators

Before you begin

  • An authorized Unit session and reviewed indoor map, floor image and saved regions prepared for your department.
  • Use a fictional practice map for this exercise; verify operational site information with its owner.

Chapters

  1. Open the indoor reference map
  2. Select a saved room and read its details
  3. Switch layers and inspect an imported room
  4. Filter the visible region overlays
  5. Clear the search and restore the layer view
  6. Review imported KML and KMZ areas
  7. Reopen the map and explain its operational use

Watch the walkthrough

Download video · Download captions

Step 1

Open the indoor reference map

In Unit, open Maps and find the Community coordination centre under Indoor Maps. Select that indoor entry. The viewer opens with Ground floor selected in blue. The row beneath the Indoor Maps heading lets you switch between this floor plan and the department’s other saved layers. This fictional example is for reviewing reference information with a crew.

Practice

Open the indoor reference map

Step 2

Select a saved room and read its details

On Ground floor, inspect the floor-plan image and the shaded room area. Select that shaded area. A card opens with the saved name, Briefing room, and its description. Read the card before relying on a short map label. The image and the selectable room overlay are separate pieces of reference information that should agree with the reviewed plan.

Practice

Select a saved room and read its details

Step 3

Switch layers and inspect an imported room

Select the G I S import tab. This layer shows two imported region overlays on the floor image. Select the training-room area to open its saved Training room import details. Changing a tab changes the layer you are inspecting. Check the selected blue tab and the opened region’s name together so you are discussing the intended reference.

Practice

Switch layers and inspect an imported room

Step 4

Filter the visible region overlays

Enter Equipment in the search field. The matching equipment overlay remains while the other region overlay is filtered out. The room names printed inside the floor-plan image remain visible because that image is separate from the searchable overlay. Select the matching shaded area to open Equipment import and review its saved description. Search helps focus the display; it does not change the saved floor drawing.

Practice

Filter the visible region overlays

Step 5

Clear the search and restore the layer view

Close the detail card and remove Equipment from the search field. Both region overlays return on the G I S import layer. Clear a search when handing the device to another crew member so the restricted view is not mistaken for the complete layer.

Practice

Clear the search and restore the layer view

Step 6

Review imported KML and KMZ areas

Select the K M L and K M Z import tab. This data layer displays the two imported areas over the base map, without the floor-plan image. Select each area and read its saved name and description. The dispatchable area also offers Dispatch to this zone. This walkthrough reviews the reference cards without submitting a call. Verify the selected area and your department’s call procedure before using a dispatch action.

Practice

Review imported KML and KMZ areas

Step 7

Reopen the map and explain its operational use

Return to Maps and reopen the same Indoor Maps entry. Ground floor and the saved room overlay return in this example. These are stored reference locations; this viewer does not show who is inside a room or confirm physical arrival. Community paramedicine and mental health teams, search-and-rescue crews, security departments, wildland contractors and industrial response teams can use reviewed site references alongside fire and E M S. Keep the map current and verify live-location information separately.
