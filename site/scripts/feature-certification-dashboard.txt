Certification Dashboard

Who holds which certifications and what is expiring.

Why it helps
See who is qualified and whose certifications are expiring before it affects who can respond.

How to configure it
Record members' current certifications, then review what expires soon.

Before you begin
Check your department access and intended audience.

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Certification Dashboard: Assign approved response and outreach training; review acknowledgments and completion separately from competency or authorization.
Search and rescue: Certification Dashboard: Assign navigation, search and equipment training; follow up on incomplete sessions.
Security companies and departments: Certification Dashboard: Assign site procedures and incident-report training; review acknowledgment and completion before assigning duties.
Wildland contractors: Certification Dashboard: Assign seasonal crew and equipment training; review completion before mobilization.
Industrial emergency response teams: Certification Dashboard: Assign facility ERT, PPE and response-procedure training; track completion and follow-up.
