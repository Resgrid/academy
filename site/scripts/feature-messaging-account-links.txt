Link a messaging-platform account

Account linking associates a member with a configured external assistant platform.

Why it helps
Account linking associates a member with a configured external assistant platform.

How to configure it
Confirm a platform is configured, generate a short-lived linking code, and complete linking in the intended platform.

Before you begin
Check your department access and intended audience.

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Link a messaging-platform account: Review eligible channels and intended team recipients before an authorized communication test; configuration alone does not prove delivery.
Search and rescue: Link a messaging-platform account: Review volunteer alert channels and inactive members; test reviewed recipients and fallback communications.
Security companies and departments: Link a messaging-platform account: Review site/patrol alert recipients and channel preferences; prove delivery through a reviewed test.
Wildland contractors: Link a messaging-platform account: Review crew communication channels and remote-connectivity limitations before mobilization.
Industrial emergency response teams: Link a messaging-platform account: Review on-duty facility recipients and specialist escalation channels; test reviewed recipients and fallback procedures.
