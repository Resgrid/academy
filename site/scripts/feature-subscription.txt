Base plans and capacity

Your plan, its personnel and unit limits, and your add-ons.

Why it helps
Choose capacity for actual department needs without inferring prices or terms.

How to configure it
Check the remaining room before adding many members or units.

Before you begin
Check your department access and intended audience.

Examples for your department
