Open Chat from the Omni IC menu to find the conversations available to your account. Read the channel name before opening it so that you can choose the right audience for the update.

Open the Academy readiness training channel. The conversation heading tells you where your update would go. A search team could use a channel like this for staging coordination, and a security team could use one for a shift handoff.

Use the message box to draft a concise update. This exercise keeps the update unsent. For a real handoff, check the channel and the information your department allows you to share before sending.

Open Notifications to check the inbox. This browser says the inbox is unavailable here. If you see this state, check which device and notification channel your department expects you to use for alerts.

Under Push Notifications in Settings, use Test alert sounds to preview a tone. Message and Notification have separate controls. This is a sound test on the current device.

In the Audio section, check Microphone and Speaker to see the selected devices. Test the audio at the desk before an industrial drill or a community standby so the operator knows which equipment is in use.
