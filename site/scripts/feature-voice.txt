Voice

Push-to-Talk voice channels.

Why it helps
Coordinate field teams through app-based voice channels.

How to configure it
Create channels and add the members who use them.

Before you begin
Push-to-Talk add-on access

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Voice: Plan operational team channels and verify device and add-on prerequisites.
Search and rescue: Voice: Plan search-team and staging channels; test approved participants and fallback radio procedures.
Security companies and departments: Voice: Plan site and patrol channels with intended memberships; test authorized device access.
Wildland contractors: Voice: Plan crew and staging channels; test connectivity and approved fallback communications.
Industrial emergency response teams: Voice: Plan facility response channels; test device access and approved fallback communications.
