Deployment Finance

Deployment records and Deployment Finance.

Why it helps
Keep deployment records and cost documentation together for review and reimbursement.

How to configure it
Record your next deployment with its personnel and apparatus.

Before you begin
Feature access enabled by your system operator

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Deployment Finance: Plan an outreach event or temporary response-coverage deployment with dates, roster and operational objectives.
Search and rescue: Deployment Finance: Plan a search deployment with staging, operational period, personnel and resource roster.
Security companies and departments: Deployment Finance: Plan event-security or temporary site coverage with an operational roster and dates.
Wildland contractors: Deployment Finance: Plan a crew assignment with operational dates and resources; review cost evidence separately from the operational roster.
Industrial emergency response teams: Deployment Finance: Plan plant turnaround or mutual-aid coverage with dates, response resources and responsible reviewers.
