Open the original call in Omni Dispatch. Before adding a record, check the call name and number so you attach it to the right incident.

Open the Records tab, then select New Record. Starting here keeps the new record linked to this call.

Choose Callback from the record list. This is the built-in system form.

The form opens with fields for the initial report, cause, location, and the people or resources involved. Fill in what applies to your department.

Enter what was first reported and the location. For this training example, a crew reported that the station exterior inspection was complete.

In Narrative, explain who you called back and what you confirmed. Then select Save and send to store the draft.

The draft opens with the saved details. Review them here, and use Save if you make a correction.

When the record is complete, confirm the attestation and choose Finalize.

Check the permanent Callback number on the finalized record.

Return to the call and open Records. The Callback is listed with its number and Finalized status, so another dispatcher can find it from the incident. A search and rescue base could use the same form to document a check-back with a field team.
