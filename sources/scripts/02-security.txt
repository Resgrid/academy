ADAPT THIS WORKFLOW TO YOUR DEPARTMENT (written companion):
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Set the local time zone for scheduled outreach visits; review whether a daily reset would incorrectly mark an occupied response team available.
Search and rescue: Choose availability reset rules that suit volunteers and overnight searches; avoid implying that a remote volunteer has arrived at staging.
Security companies and departments: Set patrol-day boundaries and display order; review staffing suppression before excluding an off-duty patrol member from alerts.
Wildland contractors: Review reset times across overnight assignments and the deployment time zone before changing contractor crew availability.
Industrial emergency response teams: Align display time with plant shifts and review automatic resets before overwriting a team engaged in an extended incident.
