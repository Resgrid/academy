Saved record reports

Saved record reports you can run again.

Why it helps
Make repeatable administrative reviews easier.

How to configure it
Know which reports your department completes and who reviews them. Records may need to be enabled for your department.

Before you begin
Feature access enabled by your system operator; Manage Record Reports permission

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Saved record reports: Use an approved operational record definition for visits or responses; select fields and access according to the service’s record policy.
Search and rescue: Saved record reports: Record search activity, participants and outcomes; distinguish a saved draft from a reviewed or finalized record.
Security companies and departments: Saved record reports: Record patrol or incident activity under an appropriate definition; review required fields and client/site access.
Wildland contractors: Saved record reports: Record assignment activity and crew evidence; link the correct call or deployment and retain review status.
Industrial emergency response teams: Saved record reports: Record drills and response activity; review the definition, participants and finalization requirements.
