Open Call Notes on the intended incident and enter the update. In this training exercise, the server connection is unavailable. Choose Add Note once so you can check what happened to that update.

The note appears with the Queued badge. Omni IC saved it on this device and still needs to send it. A search team or wildland crew can use this badge to tell which changes are waiting for a connection.

Open Storage and sync from the waiting changes indicator. Under Pending changes, this example shows one item for the call note. Retrying means the app is attempting to send it again. Once you have a connection, Retry is available for an individual item.

After the connection returns, check the same outbox. In this example, it cleared automatically and the waiting, failed and stuck counts are all zero.

Reopen Call Notes and confirm the update is still there. We also checked the note in a separate session. That gives a crew handoff or community outreach team a way to confirm the update reached the server.
