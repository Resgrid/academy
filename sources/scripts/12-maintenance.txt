ADAPT THIS WORKFLOW TO YOUR DEPARTMENT (written companion):
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Turn a response-vehicle or kit defect into a work order with an owner, state and completion evidence.
Search and rescue: Track defective radios or search equipment through review, repair and return to service.
Security companies and departments: Track patrol equipment and vehicle defects through assigned repairs and verification.
Wildland contractors: Track apparatus and tool defects with assignment impact and return-to-service evidence.
Industrial emergency response teams: Track response-equipment defects through repair ownership and verified completion.
