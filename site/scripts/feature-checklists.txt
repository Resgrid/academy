Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Checklists: Use a vehicle or operational-kit checklist; follow up on failed items and distinguish scheduled compliance from an on-demand check.
Search and rescue: Checklists: Use search-kit and vehicle readiness checks; assign failed equipment follow-up.
Security companies and departments: Checklists: Use patrol vehicle or site-opening checks; review failures and missed scheduled checks.
Wildland contractors: Checklists: Use crew and apparatus readiness checks before mobilization; create accountable defect follow-up.
Industrial emergency response teams: Checklists: Use response-kit and equipment checks; review required items and route failures to maintenance.
