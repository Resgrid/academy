Records health and prevention attachments

Records health and prevention attachment screens make retained record support and issues visible to authorized users.

Why it helps
Records health and prevention attachment screens make retained record support and issues visible to authorized users.

How to configure it
Review the records health view and attach a synthetic support file to an appropriate prevention record.

Before you begin
Check your department access and intended audience.

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Records health and prevention attachments: Use an approved operational record definition for visits or responses; select fields and access according to the service’s record policy.
Search and rescue: Records health and prevention attachments: Record search activity, participants and outcomes; distinguish a saved draft from a reviewed or finalized record.
Security companies and departments: Records health and prevention attachments: Record patrol or incident activity under an appropriate definition; review required fields and client/site access.
Wildland contractors: Records health and prevention attachments: Record assignment activity and crew evidence; link the correct call or deployment and retain review status.
Industrial emergency response teams: Records health and prevention attachments: Record drills and response activity; review the definition, participants and finalization requirements.
