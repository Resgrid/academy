Preventive maintenance

Recurring maintenance schedules.

Why it helps
Plan equipment upkeep and track corrective responsibility.

How to configure it
Schedule routine service for each apparatus.

Before you begin
Readiness Pro add-on access; Maintenance module enabled

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Preventive maintenance: Turn a response-vehicle or kit defect into a work order with an owner, state and completion evidence.
Search and rescue: Preventive maintenance: Track defective radios or search equipment through review, repair and return to service.
Security companies and departments: Preventive maintenance: Track patrol equipment and vehicle defects through assigned repairs and verification.
Wildland contractors: Preventive maintenance: Track apparatus and tool defects with assignment impact and return-to-service evidence.
Industrial emergency response teams: Preventive maintenance: Track response-equipment defects through repair ownership and verified completion.
