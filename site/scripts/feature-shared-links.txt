Shared links

Share selected calls, units and personnel with a linked department.

Why it helps
Review exposed data and link access before distribution.

How to configure it
Gather the documents and contacts members need most, and decide who keeps them current.

Before you begin
Check your department access and intended audience.

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Shared links: Organize approved response and referral procedures with responsible owners and review dates.
Search and rescue: Shared links: Organize search, navigation and equipment protocols and mutual-aid references.
Security companies and departments: Shared links: Organize site instructions, escalation procedures and patrol reference documents.
Wildland contractors: Shared links: Organize assignment, equipment and agency procedures with seasonal review ownership.
Industrial emergency response teams: Shared links: Organize facility response plans, access instructions and equipment procedures with current owners.
