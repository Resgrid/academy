AI dispatch settings and activity

The checkout contains gated AI dispatch settings for enrichment fields, confidence, sender scope, token limits, and activity.

Why it helps
The checkout contains gated AI dispatch settings for enrichment fields, confidence, sender scope, token limits, and activity.

How to configure it
Confirm host enablement and department rollout before reviewing the settings. Saving settings alone does not start enrichment.

Before you begin
Check your department access and intended audience.

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: AI dispatch settings and activity: Ask for help locating setup or operational information, then check it on the owning screen; the assistant is not clinical decision support.
Search and rescue: AI dispatch settings and activity: Ask how to locate search-team or configuration information; verify the answer and current source records.
Security companies and departments: AI dispatch settings and activity: Ask for help with patrol workflow configuration; inspect site scope and source data before acting.
Wildland contractors: AI dispatch settings and activity: Ask how to find deployment or crew setup information; verify current assignment data and provider availability.
Industrial emergency response teams: AI dispatch settings and activity: Ask for help finding facility workflows; validate answers against current records and approved procedures.
