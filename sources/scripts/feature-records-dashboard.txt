ADAPT THIS WORKFLOW TO YOUR DEPARTMENT
Firehouse 51 is synthetic demonstration data. Apply the same workflow to your department’s procedures.

Community paramedicine and mental health response: Records Dashboard: Use an approved operational record definition for visits or responses; select fields and access according to the service’s record policy.

Search and rescue: Records Dashboard: Record search activity, participants and outcomes; distinguish a saved draft from a reviewed or finalized record.

Security companies and departments: Records Dashboard: Record patrol or incident activity under an appropriate definition; review required fields and client/site access.

Wildland contractors: Records Dashboard: Record assignment activity and crew evidence; link the correct call or deployment and retain review status.

Industrial emergency response teams: Records Dashboard: Record drills and response activity; review the definition, participants and finalization requirements.

Practice: reproduce the configuration in your training department, save it, inspect the result, and identify the responsible reviewer. Sector examples are written companions; their presence does not certify provider delivery, clinical scope, or an updated video export.
