Make alerts reach the intended audience
Welcome to Resgrid Academy. In this lesson, we will make alerts reach the intended audience. Repeated messages create noise, while incomplete contacts and device preferences can leave intended recipients unreachable. We will explain the features, the setup decisions, and a practical way to check the result.

A department scenario
An administrator creates notification rules for new calls, builds an Officers distribution list and runs a communication test before going live. A dispatch only helps if it reaches people. This is where you confirm members can actually be contacted. The examples in this series use a fictional department and are teaching scenarios, not default settings.

Notification rules
Notification rules. Rules that alert chosen people when calls, statuses or other events happen. Create notification rules for the events officers and administrators need to know about.

Distribution lists
Distribution lists. Reusable recipient lists, including email lists. Create lists for groups you message often, such as Officers.

Communication Tests
Communication Tests. Test messages that show whether members can be reached. Run a communication test before going live and after major changes.

Chat
Chat. Team chat for members. Create channels for your teams once chat is enabled.

Inbox
Inbox. Messages sent to you. Make sure members have added a mobile number or email address and installed the Responder app. Availability also depends on Messaging. Check access in the target department.

Sent Messages
Sent Messages. Messages you have sent. Make sure members have added a mobile number or email address and installed the Responder app. Availability also depends on Messaging. Check access in the target department.

New Message
New Message. Send a message, poll or callback request to members. Make sure members have added a mobile number or email address and installed the Responder app. Availability also depends on Messaging. Check access in the target department.

Chatbot and Assistant integration settings
Chatbot and Assistant integration settings. Connect the Resgrid Assistant to chat platforms and choose what it may do. Make sure members have added a mobile number or email address and installed the Responder app.

Link a messaging-platform account
Account linking associates a member with a configured external assistant platform. Confirm a platform is configured, generate a short-lived linking code, and complete linking in the intended platform. Review the linked identity and a controlled permitted action. Keep the temporary code out of the recording. Confirm availability in the demo department before recording the live workflow.

Configure one useful workflow
For the training scenario, work in this order. Step 1. Confirm member contacts and notification preferences. Step 2. Create an Officers distribution list and a focused notification rule. Step 3. Run an explicit communication test and review the recorded outcomes.

Verify the result
Now verify the result. Ask test recipients to confirm the actual channel and time received. Review missing or failed results and the intended audience. A common problem to watch for: A provider handoff is not confirmed delivery. Staffing suppression, recipient selection, and contact eligibility each deserve a separate check.

Try it with your department
Your practice task is simple. Send only to the demo audience. Record recipients, observed receipts, failures, and follow-up actions; compare with the expected audience. Record the result, who owns the follow-up, and when it should be reviewed.

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Review eligible channels and intended team recipients before an authorized communication test; configuration alone does not prove delivery.
Search and rescue: Review volunteer alert channels and inactive members; test reviewed recipients and fallback communications.
Security companies and departments: Review site/patrol alert recipients and channel preferences; prove delivery through a reviewed test.
Wildland contractors: Review crew communication channels and remote-connectivity limitations before mobilization.
Industrial emergency response teams: Review on-duty facility recipients and specialist escalation channels; test reviewed recipients and fallback procedures.
