Deployment From External Order

Create a deployment from a mutual-aid resource order.

Why it helps
Keep deployment records and cost documentation together for review and reimbursement.

How to configure it
Useful when your members deploy to incidents outside your jurisdiction.

Before you begin
Feature access enabled by your system operator

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Deployment From External Order: Plan an outreach event or temporary response-coverage deployment with dates, roster and operational objectives.
Search and rescue: Deployment From External Order: Plan a search deployment with staging, operational period, personnel and resource roster.
Security companies and departments: Deployment From External Order: Plan event-security or temporary site coverage with an operational roster and dates.
Wildland contractors: Deployment From External Order: Plan a crew assignment with operational dates and resources; review cost evidence separately from the operational roster.
Industrial emergency response teams: Deployment From External Order: Plan plant turnaround or mutual-aid coverage with dates, response resources and responsible reviewers.
