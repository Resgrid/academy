Audience: Members, dispatchers, and commanders

Put the right Resgrid app on each device
Welcome to Resgrid Academy. In this lesson, we will put the right resgrid app on each device. A configured department still needs members and shared devices to sign in to the correct department with the correct permissions. We will explain the features, the setup decisions, and a practical way to check the result.

A department scenario
Members install the Responder app, a tablet in Engine 1 runs the Unit app and a station screen shows the Big Board. Most members use Resgrid through the Responder app. Getting everyone installed and signed in is what lets dispatches reach them. The examples in this series use a fictional department and are teaching scenarios, not default settings.

Responder application
Responder application. The member app for receiving dispatches, responding and setting status. Have every member install the Responder app, sign in and allow notifications.

Unit application
Unit application. The in-vehicle app that represents an apparatus. Install the Unit app on each apparatus tablet and select its unit.

Dispatch application
Dispatch application. The dispatcher view for creating and managing calls. Give dispatch access to the people who dispatch, then have them sign in.

Incident command and accountability
Incident command and accountability. Incident command and accountability for incident commanders. Give command access to the officers who run incidents.

Big Board
Big Board. A station display of calls, units and personnel. Open the Big Board on a station screen and choose what it shows.

Dashboard
Dashboard. The department home page: current status, staffing and activity. Decide which devices each role will use and share the app download links with your members.

My Profile
My Profile. Your own profile, contact details and notification preferences. Decide which devices each role will use and share the app download links with your members.

Your Departments
Your Departments. Switch between the departments you belong to. Decide which devices each role will use and share the app download links with your members.

Configure one useful workflow
For the training scenario, work in this order. Step 1. Set up a member device with Responder and notification permissions. Step 2. Set up an apparatus tablet with Unit and select its unit. Step 3. Grant appropriate Dispatch or Command access and configure a station Big Board.

Verify the result
Now verify the result. Check the active department, device identity, and a controlled notification test. Check what a shared station screen exposes. A common problem to watch for: Installing an app does not prove alerts arrive. Verify OS notification and location settings on the actual device used.

Try it with your department
Your practice task is simple. Use a sample member, unit tablet, dispatcher, and station display to follow the same training incident. Record which devices complete the test. Record the result, who owns the follow-up, and when it should be reviewed.

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Choose member, dispatch and display apps according to response-team duties; verify device registration and external providers separately.
Search and rescue: Choose field-member, dispatch and coordination-display apps; verify location update and remote connectivity.
Security companies and departments: Choose patrol-member, dispatcher and site-display apps; verify site scope and device registration.
Wildland contractors: Choose crew-member, dispatch and coordination apps; verify device updates during remote assignments.
Industrial emergency response teams: Choose responder, control-room and display apps; verify provider prerequisites and facility connectivity.
