Open Deployments in Omni Dispatch. The Open filter gives you the deployments that are still in progress. Select Community support deployment and check its status and assigned resource counts. A wildland contractor could use this view to confirm which deployment a crew is working under.

Use Previous day to move to the day you are reviewing. This exercise has a crew report on November second. Reports this day shows that the entry is Approved and totals eleven and a half hours. Open the report to check the individual entries and their time ranges and breaks. The fields in this approved report are read-only.

On the Dispatch Console, open the warehouse exercise and select Command. The command summary names the commander and shows the structure. In this example, Squad Three is in Search Division. Use View Squad Three to see the lane assignment and its requirements warning. A search and rescue dispatcher could use the same view to confirm which group a search team is working with.

Check Accountability to see the current check-in indicators. Objectives shows the progress of the assigned benchmark. The separate Check-In tab shows the timer and last check-in for a tracked entry. Confirm the entry before using the check-in action.

For documentation tied to the call, open its Records tab and select New Record. The form list shows the choices available to Dispatch. This training department shows Callback and Run. The department Records workspace also groups items under Assigned to you, Drafts and Returned to you. Use the call tab when you need the context of a particular incident, and the workspace to review your queue.
