AI assistance beyond the free Admin Assist allowance.

Why it helps
Help explain approved evidence and prepare drafts for human review when released.

How to configure it
Nothing to set up yet; your free Admin Assist allowance works without it.

Enhanced AI add-on access

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Enhanced AI: Ask for help locating setup or operational information, then check it on the owning screen; the assistant is not clinical decision support.
Search and rescue: Enhanced AI: Ask how to locate search-team or configuration information; verify the answer and current source records.
Security companies and departments: Enhanced AI: Ask for help with patrol workflow configuration; inspect site scope and source data before acting.
Wildland contractors: Enhanced AI: Ask how to find deployment or crew setup information; verify current assignment data and provider availability.
Industrial emergency response teams: Enhanced AI: Ask for help finding facility workflows; validate answers against current records and approved procedures.
