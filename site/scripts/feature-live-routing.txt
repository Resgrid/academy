Live Routing

Directions and routes for responding resources.

Why it helps
A current map shows where responders and apparatus are and puts important locations in front of dispatchers.

How to configure it
Confirm members allow location sharing in the Responder app, and decide how long a position should stay on the map.

Before you begin
Mapping module enabled

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Live Routing: Use current operational team positions for visit coverage; choose a location lifetime and follow the service’s location-access procedures.
Search and rescue: Live Routing: Set a location lifetime suitable for remote search updates; use search-area POIs and distinguish stale positions from current team location.
Security companies and departments: Live Routing: Map patrol vehicles and site POIs; review stale-location lifetime before treating an old position as current patrol coverage.
Wildland contractors: Live Routing: Map staging, divisions and support vehicles; review device update frequency and stale location during remote assignments.
Industrial emergency response teams: Live Routing: Map facility access points, response equipment and team positions; review indoor or weak-signal limitations and fallback procedures.
