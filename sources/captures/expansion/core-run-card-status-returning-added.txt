How dispatchable statuses work
Most run cards can leave this tab empty. With nothing selected the card uses the standard availability rules: units and personnel whose status counts as available or delayed, and staffing levels that count as available, on shift or delayed.
Selecting anything in a section replaces the standard rules for that section: only what you select counts as available, and everything else is skipped.
To widen the standard rules, click "Start from the standard rules" and then tick the extra statuses, for example Returning, so units heading back from another call can still be recommended.
Prefer status meanings to individual statuses. A meaning matches every status with that meaning, built-in or your own, including statuses you add later.
