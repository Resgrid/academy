Omni Dispatch: review incident command, check-ins and deployments

Review an approved crew report, check command assignments and accountability, and locate call-linked Records forms.

Audience: Dispatchers, Department administrators

Before you begin

  • Omni Dispatch and an account in a training department with permission for the screens shown.

Chapters

  1. Review deployment status and approved crew time
  2. Review command assignments and check-ins
  3. Find call-linked forms and the Records queue

Watch the walkthrough

Download video · Download captions

Step 1

Review deployment status and approved crew time

Open Deployments in Omni Dispatch. The Open filter gives you the deployments that are still in progress. Select Community support deployment and check its status and assigned resource counts. A wildland contractor could use this view to confirm which deployment a crew is working under. Use Previous day to move to the day you are reviewing. This exercise has a crew report on November second. Reports this day shows that the entry is Approved and totals eleven and a half hours. Open the report to check the individual entries and their time ranges and breaks. The fields in this approved report are read-only.

Practice

Review deployment status and approved crew time in a fictional training department.

Step 2

Review command assignments and check-ins

On the Dispatch Console, open the warehouse exercise and select Command. The command summary names the commander and shows the structure. In this example, Squad Three is in Search Division. Use View Squad Three to see the lane assignment and its requirements warning. A search and rescue dispatcher could use the same view to confirm which group a search team is working with. Check Accountability to see the current check-in indicators. Objectives shows the progress of the assigned benchmark. The separate Check-In tab shows the timer and last check-in for a tracked entry. Confirm the entry before using the check-in action.

Practice

Review command assignments and check-ins in a fictional training department.

Step 3

Find call-linked forms and the Records queue

For documentation tied to the call, open its Records tab and select New Record. The form list shows the choices available to Dispatch. This training department shows Callback and Run. The department Records workspace also groups items under Assigned to you, Drafts and Returned to you. Use the call tab when you need the context of a particular incident, and the workspace to review your queue.

Practice

Find call-linked forms and the Records queue in a fictional training department.
