Group-Scoped Dispatch
Scope Dispatch to Group Areas
Limit supported dispatch views to the member's group subtree. Department administrators and configured department-wide roles keep department-wide access.
When on, each member's active call lists, maps and nearest unit board show only their own group and the groups beneath it: calls inside those groups' boundaries or dispatched to them, plus any call the member reported or was dispatched to. Department admins and the roles below keep a department-wide view. Nothing is copied or moved, so handing dispatch to another desk at shift change is only a change of role.

In Quarters
Set, by meaning in the same way, on the units still working a call when it is closed or they are taken off it. A unit already back in service, out of service (unavailable, maintenance or on a break) or working another call keeps its status; custom statuses are read by their base type. Default sets the unit's back-in-service status (Cleared, Available or In Quarters) on custom statuses and Released on the built-in statuses.
Personnel on Unit Set Unit Status
Allow personnel status updates to influence their assigned unit status. Review automation and crew assignment together.
When a unit submits a status and users are setup on roles, the users status will be set to On Unit.
Hold to set status
Unit and Responder apps set a status with a two-second press and hold instead of a tap followed by Next or Submit. Statuses that need a note or destination still open that step.
In the Unit and Responder apps, crews press and hold a status button for two seconds to set it, instead of tapping it and then pressing Next or Submit. Prevents accidental taps and forgotten confirmations. A status that needs a note or a destination choice still opens its next step.
Close call when last unit clears
An active call closes on its own when its last dispatched unit reports back in service or out of service. Units still working, a call with no unit dispatched and a call under an active incident command keep it open.
An active call closes on its own when the last unit dispatched to it reports back in service or out of service (a status whose base type is Available, In Quarters, Cleared, Unavailable or Maintenance). Every other dispatched unit must also have reported finished since its dispatch. Only units count: a call with no unit dispatched, a call under an active incident command, or a unit still returning or transporting keeps the call open for the dispatcher.

Order by driving ETA
Re-rank the closest candidates using routed drive time instead of straight-line distance. Uses the mapping provider and may add cost and latency.
ETA shortlist size
In-quarters turnout (seconds)
Time a unit at its station needs before it is moving. Applies to units in an In Quarters status and to units with no GPS position (taken to be at their station). Added to the travel time when ranking closest units. 0 = none.
Mobile turnout (seconds)
Time a unit that is already out and available needs before it is moving to a new call. Applies to units ranked from their GPS position. Added to the travel time when ranking closest units. 0 = none.
Personnel turnout (seconds)
Added to a person's travel time when run card rows that accept people compare them with units.
Dispatch freed units to calls awaiting them
Off: dispatchers are alerted when a matching unit becomes available. On: the unit is sent to the highest-priority waiting call automatically. Run cards can override this.
Automatic dispatch limit (seconds, 0 = none)
A freed unit further away than this is alerted, not sent. The alert itself always fires.
