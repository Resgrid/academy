Hydrants and water sources

Hydrants and water sources with their inspection and flow history.

Why it helps
Identify records needing verification without claiming current flow or availability.

How to configure it
Know which reports your department completes and who reviews them. Records may need to be enabled for your department.

Before you begin
Feature access enabled by your system operator
