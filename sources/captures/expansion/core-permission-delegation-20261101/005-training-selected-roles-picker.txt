Advanced Data Protection
When the Advanced Data Protection add-on is active, these two permissions decide who may receive protected call details by SMS or voice after entering their PIN, and who may change how protected content leaves Resgrid. Revealing or editing protected data on screen follows each page's own access rules plus a recent two-factor verification. Unlike most Resgrid permissions, unset values default to the selection shown.
View Protected Call Data	Who may receive protected call details (nature, address, contact info, notes) by SMS or voice after entering their PIN, when PIN release is turned on. On-screen and app reveal follows call access plus two-factor verification. Defaults to Everyone because responding personnel must be able to read a dispatch.
