Shifts

Shift schedules, sign-ups and trades.

Why it helps
Shifts show who is on duty and can drive dispatch, so calls page the people who are actually working.

How to configure it
Create your shifts, assign groups and members, and decide whether dispatch follows shifts.

Before you begin
Shifts module enabled

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Shifts: Schedule mobile-crisis or outreach coverage windows and eligible team roles; review gaps and overlapping assignments.
Search and rescue: Shifts: Schedule search standby and field-team coverage with eligible roles; do not confuse sign-up with arrival at staging.
Security companies and departments: Shifts: Schedule site patrol coverage and supervisor positions; review whether members may sign up for multiple shift groups.
Wildland contractors: Shifts: Schedule crew availability and relief windows; check conflicts with deployments and rest requirements.
Industrial emergency response teams: Shifts: Schedule facility ERT coverage across plant shifts; review required positions and overlapping assignments.
