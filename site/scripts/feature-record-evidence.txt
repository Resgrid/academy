Evidence capture and provenance

Collect and track evidence attached to a record.

Why it helps
Preserve evidence context and access restrictions without copying it into setup guidance.

How to configure it
Know which reports your department completes and who reviews them. Records may need to be enabled for your department.

Before you begin
Feature access enabled by your system operator

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Evidence capture and provenance: Use an approved operational record definition for visits or responses; select fields and access according to the service’s record policy.
Search and rescue: Evidence capture and provenance: Record search activity, participants and outcomes; distinguish a saved draft from a reviewed or finalized record.
Security companies and departments: Evidence capture and provenance: Record patrol or incident activity under an appropriate definition; review required fields and client/site access.
Wildland contractors: Evidence capture and provenance: Record assignment activity and crew evidence; link the correct call or deployment and retain review status.
Industrial emergency response teams: Evidence capture and provenance: Record drills and response activity; review the definition, participants and finalization requirements.
