Map Icon Legend
Home  Mapping  Map Icon Legend

Every icon the map shows for calls, units and personnel. A call's icon follows its type and priority, a unit's its type and status, and a member's their role and status. Staffing levels never change an icon.

How an icon is picked
An icon uploaded for the type or role for that exact priority or status.
Otherwise the type's or role's own uploaded icon, with a badge in the color of the priority or status.
Otherwise the built-in pin in the color of the priority or status.

A priority or status with no meaning set is colored by what its name suggests, such as Emergency or Out of Service; one whose name suggests nothing shows a gray pin, as does a unit or member that has set no status. Set meanings on the Custom Statuses and Call Priorities pages to be sure.
