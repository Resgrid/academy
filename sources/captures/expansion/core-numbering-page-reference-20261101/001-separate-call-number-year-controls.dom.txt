  - heading "Call Numbering" [level=3]
  - paragraph: How new calls are numbered. Changing the pattern never renumbers calls that already have a number.
  - generic: Call number pattern
  - textbox "Call number pattern": "FD51-{YYYY}-{SEQ}"
  - paragraph:
    - text: "Example:"
    - code: FD51-2026-0153
  - generic: "How call numbers are written, for example FD-2026-0153: fixed text plus the YYYY or YY, MM, DD and SEQ tokens. The sequence starts again whenever the date it writes changes. Until a pattern is saved, calls keep the YY-SEQ numbers Resgrid has always issued."
  - generic: "Fixed text (letters, digits, - _ . /) around tokens: {YYYY} or {YY} the year, {MM} the month, {DD} the day and {SEQ} the sequence, which must appear once. The sequence starts again at 1 whenever the date in the number changes: each year with a year, each month with {MM}, each day with {DD}, and never without a date. {MM} needs a year and {DD} needs {MM}. Dates use the department's time zone."
  - generic: Sequence digits
  - spinbutton "Sequence digits": "4"
  - generic: Pads the sequence with leading zeros, so 4 writes 0153. 1 writes it without padding, as Resgrid always has.
  - generic: "Digits in the SEQ part of the call number, zero-padded: 4 writes 0153. 1 to 8 digits, 1 for no padding; changing it keeps the sequence's next number."
