Open the Shared device settings at sign-in and enter a recognizable device label. This fictional dispatch workstation is the Academy Dispatch handoff desk. Choose Save.

Check the saved label on the sign-in form. Each dispatcher uses their own account so the application can track the active operator.

During the shift, open Settings and find Session. Choose Lock when you step away from the workstation. The same step can help at a shared security desk or community coordination center.

The locked screen shows which operator is signed in. This example has no eligible verification method. Choose End shift to return to sign-in, or use Switch operator when another operator is taking over.

Verify that the sign-in form returns and the desk label is still visible. The next operator can now sign in for their own shift.
