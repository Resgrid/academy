Quality review and sampling

Post-finalization review uses rubrics, sampling, scores, and trends to identify reporting follow-up.

Why it helps
Post-finalization review uses rubrics, sampling, scores, and trends to identify reporting follow-up.

How to configure it
Confirm rollout and reviewer access, configure an approved rubric, sample finalized records, and score a demo review.

Before you begin
Check your department access and intended audience.

Examples for your department
Community paramedicine and mental health response: Quality review and sampling: Use an approved operational record definition for visits or responses; select fields and access according to the service’s record policy.
Search and rescue: Quality review and sampling: Record search activity, participants and outcomes; distinguish a saved draft from a reviewed or finalized record.
Security companies and departments: Quality review and sampling: Record patrol or incident activity under an appropriate definition; review required fields and client/site access.
Wildland contractors: Quality review and sampling: Record assignment activity and crew evidence; link the correct call or deployment and retain review status.
Industrial emergency response teams: Quality review and sampling: Record drills and response activity; review the definition, participants and finalization requirements.
